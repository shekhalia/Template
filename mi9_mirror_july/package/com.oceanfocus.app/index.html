    Official description

    Ocean – Build Deep Focus. Flow Every Day.

    Ocean is a calm, powerful productivity companion designed to help you build consistent deep work habits — one focused session at a time.

    Whether you're a student, professional, or creative, Ocean gives you everything you need to stay on track, build momentum, and grow your productivity over time.

    ⏱ Pomodoro Focus Timer
    Choose from 15, 25, 45, or 60-minute focus sessions. Link each session to a specific task for precise tracking and intentional work.

    ✅ Smart Task Management
    Create tasks with categories, priorities, and schedules. Mark them complete and review your history grouped by day.

    🔥 Daily Streaks
    Hit your daily focus goal to build a streak. Your best streak is tracked all-time — your progress is never lost.

    🏅 Achievements & Badges
    Unlock 25+ badges across sessions, minutes, tasks, and streaks. Always see exactly how close you are to your next achievement.

    📊 Focus Stats & Insights
    Track your daily, weekly, and all-time focus minutes. See your top task, session count, and level progress at a glance.

    🏆 Leaderboard
    See how your focus time compares to others this week. Stay motivated with a community leaderboard updated in real-time.

    🔔 Smart Reminders
    Get task reminders and a daily summary notification so your goals are always top of mind.

    🎵 Ambient Sounds
    Focus better with built-in ocean waves, rain, and forest sounds — no headphones required.

    🌙 Dark & Light Mode
    A beautiful ocean-blue theme in both light and dark modes, designed to keep you calm and in the zone.

    ☁️ Cloud Sync
    Your stats, tasks, and progress sync securely across devices — pick up exactly where you left off.

    Free. No ads. No subscriptions.
    Ocean is completely free to use with no hidden costs, no ads, and no premium paywalls.

    Download Ocean today and start building the focus habit that changes everything. 🌊

    Requires Android 8.0 or later. Internet connection needed for cloud sync and leaderboard features.
