    Official description

    You wake up alone. This zombie survival game begins where the last day on earth ends - and the zombie apocalypse didn't announce itself. It just arrived.

    You open your eyes to ash and silence. No phone signal. No map. No memory of how long you've been unconscious in the middle of this wasteland. Then you find the radio. A voice crackles through the static. There are others. And they need you.

    Rise of Apocalypse is an open-world zombie survival game set in the ruins of civilization. Your mission unfolds across dangerous territories: discover what triggered the zombie apocalypse, track down survivors scattered across the wasteland, and uncover the truth buried inside military bunkers, abandoned hospitals, and ruined roads that no living person has walked in months. The days after the outbreak feel endless - but somewhere in the dark, answers are waiting.

    EXPLORE A SHATTERED WORLD
    Travel through a vast post-apocalyptic landscape filled with unique locations. Every dawn of zombies brought new horrors to these places: sealed military bunkers, overgrown highways, collapsed urban districts, and eerie field hospitals frozen in the moment everything stopped. Every location hides resources, stories, and clues. Quest deeper into the wasteland and the truth gets darker.

    BUILD YOUR SHELTER AND DEFEND YOUR TERRITORY
    In the days after civilization collapsed, a base is the only thing standing between you and the dead. Gather wood and stone. Construct walls, storage, and living quarters. Expand and reinforce until your shelter becomes a true sanctuary. Every upgrade changes how you approach the next leg of your journey through this zombie survival game.

    CRAFT WEAPONS, ARMOR, AND ESSENTIAL GEAR
    Scavenge raw materials and turn them into everything you need to stay alive. Forge melee weapons and improvised firearms. Shoot your way through infected zones or move through them unseen - how you handle combat is your choice. Survival in a zombie apocalypse survival game means adapting, not just fighting.

    LEVEL UP YOUR CHARACTER AND GROW YOUR SKILLS
    Develop your abilities across combat, crafting, exploration, and resource management. Each decision shapes the kind of survivor you become. Who do you trust? What do you sacrifice? How many days after the last day on earth can you endure before the truth finally surfaces?

    TRADE, NEGOTIATE, AND BUILD ALLIANCES
    The survivors you contact by radio are potential allies, traders, and sources of critical information. Exchange supplies and resources. Some of them have been counting every dawn of zombies since the outbreak began - and they remember things that could change everything.

    SUSTAIN YOURSELF WITH FOOD, WATER, AND FARMING
    Manage your hunger and thirst as you adventure across the wasteland. Plant and tend a small garden to grow food between dangerous expeditions. Locate and store clean water before your reserves run dry. This is a survival game where resource management is the difference between life and death - not just the next fight.

    UNRAVEL THE MYSTERY OF THE OUTBREAK
    This zombie apocalypse survival game is built around a question no one has answered yet: what really caused it? Every bunker you breach contains logs, records, and evidence. Every survivor carries a piece of the story. The apocalypse did not happen by accident. Neither did you surviving it.

    Rise of Apocalypse is a zombie survival game where every dawn of zombies is a reminder of what was lost and every step forward is a choice to keep going. Face the 7 days to die for in the opening hours. Then face the weeks, the months, and the truth.

    The last day on earth already happened. This zombie survival game doesn't end until you find the answers. The zombie apocalypse took everything - the days after are yours to reclaim.
