    Official description

    Midnight Cafe is a cozy visual novel and relaxing cafe story game. Inherit a quiet little coffee shop, open its doors late at night, and meet strangers who came for more than caffeine — they came for someone who will listen.

    Can a single cup of coffee change a person's life? In this heartfelt interactive story, you are the barista the night needs: a keeper of secrets and a maker of memories.

    ☕ BREW THE PERFECT DRINK
    Choose your ingredients: rich coffee, fine teas, honey, cardamom, rose water. Every customer has a unique taste, and the right drink might be the key that unlocks their heart.

    🌙 A COZY STORY GAME ABOUT REAL LIVES
    Live a 14-night interactive story with an unforgettable cast: a mysterious passerby, a sorrowful soul seeking comfort, a thinker lost for words. Slice-of-life storytelling at its most human.

    💬 CHOICES THAT MATTER
    Your decisions shape every conversation and every customer's path. In this visual novel, listening is a choice too.

    🕯️ COZY & RELAXING
    Calming visuals, an immersive soundtrack, auto-save, and 100% offline play: your quiet night cafe is always open, no wifi needed. No ads, no paywalls — just the story.

    🌍 7 LANGUAGES
    English, Arabic, Turkish, French, Russian, Spanish, and Brazilian Portuguese.

    If you love visual novels, cozy games, cafe stories, or soul-touching slice-of-life experiences far from loud action games, Midnight Cafe was made for you. Open the doors. The night is heavier than you think — and a single cup of coffee might be all someone needs to start anew.
