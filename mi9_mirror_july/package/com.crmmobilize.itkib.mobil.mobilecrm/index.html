    RandevuCRM is a modern appointment and customer management platform developed for businesses that operate by appointment.

    Designed for hairdressers, beauty salons, clinics, and service-oriented businesses, RandevuCRM allows you to:

    • Easily manage your appointments
    • Gather customer information in one place
    • Send SMS and WhatsApp reminders
    • Add or edit your SMS and WhatsApp templates. • View performance reports
    • Digitize your workflow
    • Instantly prepare your offers and send them with a single click through any preferred communication channel.

    RandevuCRM helps your business run more smoothly and reduces customer churn.

    Its simple, fast, and mobile-focused design makes it easy to use on tablets or phones.
