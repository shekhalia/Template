    Official description

    Black Queen is a ruthless rummy-style card game. Build sets to score points, but get stuck holding the Black Queen of Spades when a round ends, and it costs you 100 brutal points.

    Face three AI opponents with completely different (and deeply annoying) strategies. One counts every card and grabs the discard pile the instant the math favors it. One hoards cards for ages, then drops a massive set when you’re least prepared. One plays pure chaos and still pulls wins that feel rigged. They talk during the hand. They complain about their cards when they’re winning.

    Play online with friends instead. Quick Match or custom tables, singles or teams. Invite via email, username, contacts, or Play Games. (In teams, real partners play without strategy chat — the AIs are the only ones allowed to complain.)

    A fresh daily hand drops every midnight. Same deal for everyone, with global and friends leaderboards plus a streak counter for the days you actually show up.

    Progress, stats, achievements, and XP sync across devices. Three themes. Sign in to keep everything in sync.

    There are no friends or family in a game of cards.
