    Official description

    Stay connected to your AI agents wherever you are. Use the Autohive mobile app to collaborate with your agents and manage action requests on the go.

    What you can do with Autohive in your pocket:

    - View all your workspaces and agents in one place
    - Access your complete conversation history, synced between desktop and mobile
    - Connect with your agents when you’re away from your desk
    - Use voice-to-text to chat effortlessly on the move
    - Get instant notifications for agent action requests
    - Approve or decline action requests to keep your work flowing

    Getting started is simple: sign in with your existing Autohive account to find all your workspaces and agents ready to go, and start collaborating and managing action requests without missing a beat. With mobile access you’re always connected to your AI agents, so you can approve a vendor order or chat with your sales agent right from your phone.

    Download the Autohive mobile app today and take your agents with you.
    (Requires an existing Autohive account)
