    Official description

    Home Asset Melbet is designed for homeowners and property managers to keep track of valuable assets across multiple properties. Whether it's a primary residence, rental apartment, or farmhouse, users can log every appliance, electronic, and furniture item with purchase details, photo, warranty expiry dates, and full service history. The app shows the warranty date clearly so you can easily see if it is still valid or expired. With a clean interface, summary, and all data stored privately on-device, this makes home asset management simple, organized, and always accessible without needing an internet connection.
