    Official description

    Test your reflexes and focus with Gravity Ball – a simple yet incredibly addictive minimalist arcade game!
    The core mechanic is straightforward: just tap the screen to make the ball bounce, change direction, and zig-zag between the left and right walls. However, the path ahead is filled with dangerous obstacles and shifting traps. One wrong tap, one split-second distraction, and it's game over!
    🌟 KEY FEATURES:
    Minimalist One-Tap Gameplay: Easy to pick up, challenging to master. Perfect for quick gaming sessions anywhere, anytime.
    Endless Challenge: There are no limits to your score. The longer you survive, the faster and more intense the obstacles become.
    Fast-Paced Action: Demands perfect rhythm and lightning-fast hand-eye coordination.
    Clean Visuals: Striking minimalist art style and high-contrast colors keep you focused on the action without visual clutter.
    Leaderboard Competition: Beat your own high score and challenge friends to see who can survive the longest.
