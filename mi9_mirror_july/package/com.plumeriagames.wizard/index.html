    Official description

    You might look like a warrior, but make no mistake... Wizard, Anyway!
    Equip massive greatswords or swing heavy battle-axes. It doesn't matter if everyone mistakes you for a barbarian warrior. Because you are a Wizard, anyway!

    Every time you swing your blade, chain lightning crackles, and when your axe strikes, an army of skeletons erupts! Prove your worth through the ultimate synergy of steel and spell.

    Core Gameplay Features

    1. Torrent of 60 Spells from Blades and Axes
    Equip your custom spell combos across 5 active slots. As you run through the battlefield swinging weapons, spells are automatically cast and targeted, smashing monsters to dust.
    Awaken your spells by collecting duplicate spellbooks. Evolve your spells to grant increased range, extra projectiles, massive explosion areas, and visual spell upgrades.

    2. Brand Your Gear with Modular Spell Enchant
    Brand your equipment with spell enchants to forge your own overpowered builds.
    * Weapons & Rings: Feel the impact as every weapon swing unleashes ice shards or summons skeleton warriors to protect your frontlines.
    * Shields & Necklaces: Trigger a 100% Mana Shield or an instant Full Heal when surrounded by monsters, escaping danger completely unharmed.
    * Boots & Gloves: Ignore cooldowns and resource costs upon casting, triggering consecutive Meteor and Blizzard casts that shatter the earth.
    * Scrap & Protection Runes: If an enchantment fails and gear is destroyed, recover Mana Shards to craft a Protection Rune, safeguarding your gear from future destruction.

    3. Test Your Spellpower in 5 Unique Dungeons
    Put your hybrid Battle-Mage build to the test in dungeons featuring diverse strategic rules:
    * Gold Mine: Survive the relentless onslaught of slimes and golems to plunder massive Gold.
    * Ancient Quarry: Collect essential crafting materials with drop rates that rotate and gain a 50% bonus depending on the day of the week.
    * Sage's Archive: Hunt down powerful magic bosses and seize high-circle random spellbook chests to learn high-tier spells.
    * Adventurer's Guild: Complete your bounty quotas as fast as possible while minimizing damage taken to earn the highest Gold rank.
    * Endless Spire: An energy-free endless playground! Climb to the highest floor and claim massive weekly rewards every Monday.

    4. Balanced Token Economy
    * Adventurer's Seal: Earned from daily dungeons. Exchange them directly for high-tier equipment and rare materials.
    * Magus's Sigil: Awarded for first-time stage clears and dungeon milestones. Buy spellbook chests to learn high-circle spells.

    Forget the fragile wizards who just wave wooden wands from afar. Wield heavy weapons as you summon cataclysmic storms of magic!

    Download Wizard, Anyway! today, and open your custom spellbook!
