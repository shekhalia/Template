    Official description

    Turn game day into a show. WalkUpJams is the walk-up song manager built for baseball teams — from Little League to travel ball to beer league.

    SET UP YOUR ROSTER
    Add every player with their jersey number, pick their walk-up song from Spotify or import your own audio files, and clip it to the best part. Set a batting song, a pitching entrance, or both.

    ANNOUNCE LIKE THE PROS
    Add player announcements with text-to-speech, record your own voice, or generate AI announcer voices that sound like a real stadium PA. Four announcer styles to choose from.

    ONE-TAP GAME DAY
    Tap a player in the batting order to blast their walk-up song through your Bluetooth speaker. Announcements play first, then the music kicks in. Fade out when they're set.

    SHARE WITH YOUR TEAM
    Back up your lineup to the cloud and share it with a link or share code + passcode. Other coaches or parents import the full roster — songs, clips, and announcements — with one tap.

    HYPE VIDEOS
    Generate beat-synced team intro videos with player photos, transitions, and background music. Share to social or play on the big screen.

    FREE SONGS INCLUDED
    No music subscription? Browse the built-in library of free walk-up songs, or import your own audio files.

    WORKS WITH
    • Spotify (Premium required)
    • Custom audio files (MP3, M4A, WAV)

    Built by a baseball parent who got tired of fumbling with playlists at the ballpark.

    SUBSCRIPTION DETAILS
    WalkUpJams offers two auto-renewable subscriptions billed yearly through Google Play:
    • WalkUpJams Basic — $1.99 / year. Includes Spotify playback, royalty-free song library, voice-recorded announcements, and audio file import.
    • WalkUpJams Premium — $4.99 / year. Everything in Basic plus AI voice announcements, hype videos, and cloud sync & team sharing.

    New users get a 7-day introductory window of premium-equivalent access at no charge and with no payment method required. Subscriptions auto-renew unless cancelled in Google Play at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can manage or cancel subscriptions at any time in Google Play → Subscriptions.
