    Official description

    Pop. Unscramble. Repeat.

    Word Pop is the bright, bouncy word scramble game built for quick 60-second bursts that somehow turn into an hour. Tap candy-colored letter tiles in the right order, beat the per-word timer, and watch your score explode with juicy speed bonuses.

    WHY YOU'LL LOVE IT
    • Tap-to-unscramble tiles with springy, satisfying animations
    • Progressive difficulty — climb from Sprout to Wordsmith, Maestro, Legend, and Mythic as words get longer and trickier
    • Per-word timer ring with speed bonuses — the faster you solve, the bigger the points
    • Smart hint system — first hint is free, extras cost coins (or grab a free one with a rewarded ad)
    • Daily streak with a growing flame badge — miss a day? Save your streak with one tap
    • Local Top 10 leaderboard with gold, silver, and bronze medals for podium runs
    • Pop, sparkle, and buzz sound effects + gentle haptics (both toggleable)
    • Coins economy — earn them by playing, spend them on hints and skips

    DESIGNED TO DELIGHT
    A sunny peach-to-coral gradient, floating pastel blobs, chunky, rounded tiles in mint, sky, lemon, and grape — Word Pop looks as good as it feels. Confetti bursts on every correct answer. Tile-flip animations. A friendly chunky typeface that makes every letter pop.

    PERFECT FOR
    • Commutes, coffee breaks, and queue-waiting
    • Word game fans who love Wordscapes, Word Cookies, and Anagram puzzles
    • Anyone who wants a brain workout without the stress

    100% playable offline. No account required. Free to play with optional rewarded ads if you want a boost.

    Ready to pop some words? Tap install and start your streak today.
