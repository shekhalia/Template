    Official description

    "The moment you raise the championship trophy, it is as if you are transported back twenty years. The legend of a pickleball icon begins on this very day…"

    In the game, you will step onto professional courts as a 16-year-old pickleball prodigy and start your professional career. Over the next two decades, you will compete continuously, maintain systematic training, travel to tournaments across regions, and strive to reach the pinnacle of world pickleball.
    The game features six growth systems and six major simulated tournaments. You need to set your growth path, plan your attribute progression, and prove your strength in every competition.
    You can qualify for advanced tournaments, unlock exclusive competition benefits, and receive invitations to prestigious official events. Various random emergencies and in-match variables will directly affect your career growth and competitive journey.

    【Game Features】
    1. Stat-based simulated progression with simple, user-friendly controls for effortless gameplay.
    2. Highly flexible growth strategies and multiple save slots to experience distinct pickleball career paths.
    3. Diverse professional stats, unique skills and customizable talents; freely develop and create your one-of-a-kind elite player.
    4. Compete against realistic opponents for an immersive authentic court experience; secure your playing spot and compete for top event honors.
    5. Challenge world-class pickleball tournaments, face off against top-tier competitors, and climb the global rankings.
