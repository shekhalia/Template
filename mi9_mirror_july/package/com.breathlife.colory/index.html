    Official description

    🎨 Can you see colors the way they truly are?

    ChromEyes is a relaxing yet surprisingly addictive color-guessing puzzle game. Each level shows an image with one region painted in the wrong color. Your mission: bring it back to its true shade using three simple sliders.

    HOW TO PLAY
    A zone of the picture is displayed in a false color. Slide the Red, Green and Blue channels (or switch to Hue, Saturation and Brightness) and watch the color change in real time. When it looks right, validate your guess — and discover how close you really got.

    A SCORE THAT MATCHES YOUR EYE
    ChromEyes doesn't just compare raw numbers. It uses a perceptual color-difference model (the same kind professionals use) to rate your guess from 0 to 100 and award up to 3 stars per level. The closer you get to what the human eye perceives, the higher you score.

    FEATURES
    • Dozens of handcrafted levels across multiple categories
    • Real-time color preview while you adjust the sliders
    • Two slider modes: RGB or HSB — play the way you think
    • A precise 0–100 perceptual score and 0–3 stars on every level
    • Track your best stars and aim for a perfect run
    • Color-blind assist mode with a live proximity meter
    • Clean, colorful, "mobile-game" design
    • Available in English and French

    WHY YOU'LL LOVE IT
    Whether you're an artist, a designer, a photographer, or just someone who loves a good brain teaser, ChromEyes trains your color perception in a fun, bite-sized way. Play one quick level while waiting for the bus, or chase three stars on every level — it's up to you.

    No pressure, no clutter — just you, your eyes, and the perfect color.

    Download ChromEyes now and find out how sharp your color vision really is! 🌈
