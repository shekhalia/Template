    Official description

    ComoFood – Homemade Food Marketplace

    ComoFood is a platform where users can buy and sell homemade food easily. It connects local food sellers with customers who want fresh, homemade meals at affordable prices.

    With ComoFood, sellers can create food listings, upload images, set prices, and manage orders in real-time. Buyers can browse nearby food items, place orders, track order status, and rate their experience after delivery.

    Key Features:

    • Browse homemade food from local sellers
    • Easy ordering system
    • Seller dashboard to manage food listings
    • Upload food images and descriptions
    • Real-time order status tracking
    • Rating and review system
    • Secure user authentication

    ComoFood is designed to support local home chefs and make homemade food accessible to everyone in the community.
