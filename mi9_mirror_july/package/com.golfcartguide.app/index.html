    Official description

    Cruze Cart is the ultimate navigation app built specifically for golf cart
    communities. Whether you're in Peachtree City, GA or any other golf cart-friendly
    city, Cruze Cart knows every path, tunnel, and intersection so you never get lost.

    🛺 TURN-BY-TURN GOLF CART NAVIGATION
    Get precise turn-by-turn directions that follow your community's actual cart
    path network. No more guessing which path to take — Cruze Cart knows every
    tunnel, bridge, and intersection.

    🗺️ BUILT FOR GOLF CART COMMUNITIES
    Unlike Google Maps or Apple Maps, Cruze Cart is designed specifically for golf
    cart travel. Routes stay on legal, cart-accessible paths and avoid highways
    entirely.

    🔄 AUTOMATIC REROUTING
    Take a wrong turn? No problem. Cruze Cart automatically detects when you're off
    route and recalculates in seconds.

    🛣️ SMART ROUTING
    - Stays on dedicated golf cart paths whenever possible
    - Navigates through tunnels and underpasses correctly
    - Avoids highways and cart-restricted roads
    - Accurate street and path names at every turn

    📍 PERFECT FOR
    - Golf cart community residents exploring new paths
    - Visitors and tourists navigating a new city for the first time
    - Anyone commuting by golf cart to shops, restaurants, and schools
    - New residents learning their local cart path network

    Currently available in Peachtree City, GA — the golf cart capital of the
    world — with more communities coming soon.

    Whether you're a lifelong cart path rider or brand new to golf cart living,
    Cruze Cart is your go-to navigation companion on the paths.

    Download free today and never get lost on the paths again.
