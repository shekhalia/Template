    Official description

    EduKid, the educational app that makes school fun!

    Designed by parents for children from first grade to fifth grade, EduKid
    supports your child every day with over 3,000 exercises
    aligned with the official French National Education curriculum.

    6 subjects, 40 sub-modules, thousands of questions
    • 🧮 Mathematics: addition, subtraction, multiplication, division, fractions, geometry, word problems, measurement, logic, mental arithmetic, doubles/halves, complements, proportionality
    • 📚 Reading & French: alphabet, writing, comprehension, vocabulary, grammar, conjugation, spelling, interactive dictation, phonology, high-frequency words
    • 🔬 Science: human body, animals, plants, energy, space, weather, matter, electricity, sustainable development
    • 🇬🇧 English: vocabulary, colors, family, animals, sentences, basic grammar
    • 🏛️ History & Geography: France, Europe, Prehistory, Antiquity, Middle Ages
    • 🎨 Arts: drawing, colors, music

    🎮 Multiple ways to play
    • Discovery Mode: learn at your own pace, exercises Adaptive
    • Quiz Mode: 10 questions per level (Easy / Medium / Hard)
    • 🐉 Boss Mode: 10 difficult questions + 3 lives to protect
    • ⏱ Time Trial Mode: score = correct answers × speed
    • Memory: find the matching pairs
    • Interactive Dictation with text-to-speech

    ✏️ Varied Question Types
    Multiple Choice, True/False, Fill-in-the-Blank, Audio Listening, Picture Selection, Matching Pairs, Sorting, Free-response… Your child will never get bored!

    🎯 Adapted to your child's level
    EduKid automatically detects their strengths and weaknesses. The exercises adjust in real time: too easy → the difficulty increases; too hard → it decreases. Always within the "progression zone," ensuring no frustration or demotivation.

    🏆 An experience that makes you want to come back for more
    • Points, badges, and levels to unlock
    • Fun avatars and customizable frames
    • Bronze, Silver, and Gold tiers per sub-module
    • Consecutive day streaks to maintain the habit
    • 3 daily goals that refresh every day
    • Daily challenge to add some excitement

    👥 Classmate challenges
    Your child can launch real-time challenges to their classmates:
    10 questions, the first to finish wins! Anonymous identification by
    nickname — no free chat, no contact with strangers.

    📊 Dedicated parent area
    Weekly and monthly activity reports: number of exercises,
    success rate, time spent, active days, areas for improvement.

    Locked by parent PIN.
    Adjustable daily screen time limit (30 / 45 / 60 min).

    🎤 Audio Reading
    For first and second graders who are not yet fluent readers: a button 🔊
    allows you to listen to the questions aloud. The dictation and audio questions use text-to-speech.

    🌐 Works Offline
    All exercises are available without an internet connection. The challenges and class rankings are synchronized as soon as a connection is available.

    🔒 100% Child-Friendly
    • GDPR, COPPA, and Google Play Designed for Families compliant
    • Only one non-personalized banner ad (AdMob for children) —

    never during an exercise
    • One-time purchase of "EduKid Premium" to remove ads for life
    • Purchase protected by a parent PIN
    • No advertising IDs collected
    • No free chat, no social networking
    • Clear privacy policy:

    https://hsrconsulting.github.io/edukid-privacy/

    EduKid grows with your child: starts in first grade and continues through fifth grade. One tool for all of elementary school.
