    Official description

    Cream Glass Core is a card elimination puzzle game wrapped in a warm, buttery visual style.

    Tap cards on the board to move them into your selection slot at the bottom. When three cards with the same icon line up in your slot, they vanish in a golden flash and your score climbs. Chain eliminations without pausing to build combo multipliers and rack up big points.

    But watch your slot carefully. It holds only seven cards at a time. Fill it up with no matching triple available and the round is over.

    Features

    Multiple levels with increasing card counts and icon variety
    Smooth animations and glowing transitions for every match
    Combo multiplier system that rewards consecutive clears
    Card Collection Gallery to track every icon you have encountered
    Player Statistics Panel showing your high scores, total games, and elimination history
    Daily Challenge Board for fresh puzzles every day
    Progress saved locally so you can pick up right where you left off

    The interface uses soft cream tones, peach gradients, and golden highlights to create a relaxing yet engaging atmosphere. No timers, no pressure. Just you, the cards, and a satisfying crunch each time a triple disappears.

    Designed for quick sessions or extended play. Clear the board, beat your best score, and collect them all.
