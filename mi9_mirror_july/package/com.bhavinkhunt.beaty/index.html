    Official description

    Welcome to Beaty - Fetal Heartbeat Monitor & Pregnancy Bonding Journal!

    Pregnancy is a magical journey, and hearing your baby's heartbeat for the first time is one of the most unforgettable milestones. Beaty is designed to help expecting parents connect and build a deep bond with their unborn baby in a peaceful, private, and memorable way.

    Using advanced mobile acoustic processing, Beaty captures the faint, rapid rhythmic sounds of your baby's heartbeat. You can easily record these precious moments, keep a private journal of your baby's estimated heart rate (BPM), and track their daily movements with our integrated kick counter.

    🌟 KEY FEATURES:

    • Acoustic Heartbeat Listening & Recording: Capture and save high-quality audio recordings of your baby's heartbeat directly to your device.
    • Estimated BPM Tracker: Automatically computes and displays the estimated Beats Per Minute (BPM) to keep a journal of heart rhythm trends.
    • Daily Kick Counter: Easily monitor and log your baby's movements and kicks to ensure peace of mind and keep a daily record.
    • Due Date Countdown Widget: A live countdown timer displaying weeks, days, and minutes remaining until your baby's arrival.
    • Local-First Privacy: Your personal data, recordings, and pregnancy logs are stored 100% offline on your device in a secure sandbox. We do not upload or share your sensitive information with cloud servers.

    ⚙️ HOW TO USE FOR BEST RESULTS:

    1. Find a Quiet Space: Go to a calm room and minimize background noise (turn off fans, TVs, or loud air conditioning).
    2. Remove Phone Case: Remove any protective case from your phone to ensure the microphone has direct contact.
    3. Microphone Placement: Place the bottom microphone of your phone flat against your bare lower abdomen (usually below the navel).
    4. Apply Light Pressure: Apply steady, gentle pressure to seal the microphone against the skin, which helps isolate acoustic sounds.
    5. Move Slowly: Slide the phone in small circular motions. Baby positions change frequently, so take your time to locate the sweet spot (works best after week 16-20 of pregnancy).
    6. Identify the Sound:
    - Fetal Heartbeat: Sounds like a fast, galloping horse (typically 110-160 BPM).
    - Maternal Pulse: Slower whooshing or rhythmic beat (typically 60-80 BPM).

    ⚠️ IMPORTANT MEDICAL DISCLAIMER:

    Beaty is NOT a medical device and is NOT intended to replace professional prenatal healthcare.
    • This application is designed solely for recreational bonding, journaling, and educational purposes.
    • It does not provide medical diagnoses, clinical advice, or official health measurements.
    • It should not be used as a substitute for regular checkups, clinical ultrasound scans, or monitoring by a licensed obstetrician, gynecologist, or midwife.
    • Never make medical decisions or change your pregnancy management plan based on the output of this application. If you have any concerns regarding your baby's health or movement, contact your care team or doctor immediately.
