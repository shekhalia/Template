    Official description

    Talkio is an AI-powered language learning app built for speaking practice.

    Practice real conversations anytime, improve pronunciation, and build confidence faster with instant feedback.

    What you can do with Talkio:

    Have natural voice or text conversations with AI tutors
    Get instant speaking and pronunciation feedback
    Practice with guided lessons and exercises
    Track your progress and stay consistent
    Learn and practice across many languages
    Who it’s for:

    Learners who want to speak more confidently
    Students preparing for school or exams
    Professionals improving communication for work
    Anyone who wants practical, conversation-first language learning
    Talkio helps you move from “I understand” to “I can speak.”
