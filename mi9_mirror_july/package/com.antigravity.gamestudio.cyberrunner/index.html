    Official description

    Pearl Leaf Garden

    Welcome to a tranquil botanical world where memory meets nature.

    Study the cards during the recall phase, memorize the positions of each leaf and blossom, then tap to match three identical cards before the selection bar fills up.

    How to Play
    Cards are revealed briefly at the start of each round. Once they flip over, tap cards to move them into the selection bar at the bottom. When three matching cards land in the bar, they vanish and you earn points. But be careful, if the bar fills to seven cards without a match, the round is lost.

    Progress Through the Garden
    Begin in the Seedling Garden with just a few card types. Advance through Blooming Meadow, Tropical Grove, Enchanted Forest, Crystal Canopy, and Pearl Sanctuary. Each level introduces more card varieties and longer grids, testing your memory further.

    Features
    Multiple themed levels with increasing difficulty.
    Recall phase timer that challenges your short-term memory.
    Leaf collection album to track your achievements.
    Daily bloom challenges for bonus rewards.
    Growth statistics to monitor your win streaks and lifetime matches.
    Smooth animations and a calming garden aesthetic.

    Train your memory while cultivating a beautiful digital garden. Every match brings you closer to a flourishing sanctuary.
