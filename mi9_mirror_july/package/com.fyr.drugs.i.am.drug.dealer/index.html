    Official description

    I Am Drugs Dealer is a strategic simulation game where you build your underground empire from scratch through careful planning and daily management.

    Start with a small setup and take full control of your production. Grow your plants, water them daily, and use fertilizers to speed up growth and improve quality. The better you manage your crops, the higher your profits will be.

    Once your product is ready, head to the market and make smart deals. Expand your operation, upgrade your setup, and unlock new opportunities as your business grows.

    But success isn’t easy—competition is fierce, and every decision carries risk. Stay sharp, manage wisely, and keep your operation running smoothly.
