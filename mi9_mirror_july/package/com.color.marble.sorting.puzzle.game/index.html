    Official description

    Enjoy a fun and relaxing marble sorting adventure!

    Color Marble Sort: Puzzle Game is an easy-to-play color sorting puzzle where you move marbles into the correct tubes. Use logic, plan your moves, and solve satisfying puzzles while enjoying smooth gameplay and colorful visuals.
    Each level starts with mixed marbles waiting to be sorted. Your goal is simple: place marbles of the same color together and clear the puzzle. As you progress, new challenges will test your thinking and puzzle-solving skills.

    How to Play
    🔵 Tap a tube to select a marble.
    🟢 Move it to another tube with space available.
    🟡 Match marbles of the same color.
    🟣 Sort all colors correctly to complete the level.
