    Official description

    Welcome to a delightful twist on classic mahjong puzzle games! Immerse yourself in a vibrant world filled with lush green landscapes, colorful flowers, and a serene atmosphere designed to help you completely unwind. Meet your new best friend, an incredibly cute and cheerful sheep, who will happily accompany you on this relaxing journey through the beautiful countryside.

    Experience the zen-like satisfaction of pairing gorgeous tiles featuring fresh fruits, crisp vegetables, and blooming nature. The stunning, stylized graphics bring every strawberry, carrot, and pear to life, creating a visually soothing experience that traditional puzzle lovers will absolutely adore. Whether you are looking for a nostalgic flashback to classic pair-matching mechanics or a fresh, creative approach to tile puzzles, this enchanting garden has exactly what you need.

    Dive into hundreds of captivating levels that gently challenge your mind while keeping the gameplay entirely stress-free. With engaging daily challenges and a wonderful sense of progression, you will always find a peaceful moment to escape your daily routine. Let the charming farm animals and crisp fruit tiles guide you to pure puzzle tranquility and endless enjoyment.

    FEATURES:
    - Over 200 beautifully designed levels to explore and master.
    - Adorable sheep companion and cute fruit-themed tile graphics.
    - Relaxing, zen-like atmosphere perfect for unwinding.
    - Engaging daily challenges to test your matching skills.
    - Helpful power-ups like hints, shuffles, and hammers.
    - Classic mahjong pair-matching mechanics with a creative twist.
    - Stunning vibrant backgrounds and visually satisfying animations.

    HOW TO PLAY:
    - Tap on two identical tiles featuring the same fruit or nature item to select them.
    - Ensure the chosen tiles can be connected with a line that has no more than two 90-degree turns.
    - Watch the paired tiles clear from the vibrant garden board.
    - Clear all the tiles before the time runs out to win the level and earn stars.
    - Use handy power-ups like the hammer or shuffle if you get stuck on a tricky puzzle.
