    Hexa Sort Overview

    Hexa Sort presents a straightforward color sorting puzzle where players move colored tiles between hexagonal stacks to group them. The core activity involves planning several moves ahead to clear stacks by matching colors, creating a methodical and often satisfying chain of tile merges.

    Feedback suggests the puzzle mechanics themselves are engaging, providing a calm, focused challenge. However, frequent technical issues with advertisements, including ads that freeze the game or fail to grant rewards, are a common point of frustration for players. The game's economy has also drawn criticism for increasing the cost of power ups over time.

    This title will likely appeal to players seeking a thoughtful, pattern based puzzle who can tolerate a heavy ad supported model. Key aspects include the spatial planning required by the hexagonal grid and the cascading effect of successfully clearing a stack.
