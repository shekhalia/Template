    Official description

    Russian Roulette Party is a party game designed for gatherings: fast, easy to play, and focused on local interaction between friends. Customize each player's name and avatar, pass the device around for turns, and enjoy short rounds filled with tension and laughter. Perfect for groups, dinners, and casual get-togethers.

    Key Features:

    • Fast and dynamic rounds: short matches designed for group play.
    • Customizable avatars and player names: save each player's identity during the session.
    • Retro and accessible interface: simple controls, vintage aesthetics, and instant gameplay.
    • 100% local gameplay: no registration or mandatory internet connection required (online features may be added in the future).
    • Privacy and data: only player names and avatars are used for the local gameplay experience; technical data may be collected anonymously to improve the app. No real-money gambling is involved.
    • Ready for future ads: if advertising is enabled, users will be informed and provided with customization or opt-out options as permitted by the advertising network.

    Gather your friends, spin the chamber, and discover who will be the next one selected. Every round is different, making Russian Roulette Party a simple yet exciting experience for any occasion.
