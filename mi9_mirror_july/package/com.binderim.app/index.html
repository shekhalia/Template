    Official description

    Binderim — Your Digital TCG Collection Manager

    Manage your Pokémon, One Piece, and Riftbound card collections all in one app. Save your cards, track missing ones, see your collection's value instantly, and always carry your binder with you.

    ❖ WHY BINDERIM?

    ✓ Turkish interface, designed for Turkish collectors
    ✓ Full Pokémon, One Piece, and Riftbound support
    ✓ Up-to-date database of thousands of cards
    ✓ Set-based completion tracking
    ✓ Light and dark theme support
    ✓ Mobile-friendly, fast, and simple
    ✓ Manage your card collection, buy and sell in the marketplace, and track prices.

    ❖ DIGITALIZE YOUR COLLECTION

    - Add your cards to your binder
    - Specify card status (Mint, Near Mint, Played, etc.)
    - Save foil, reverse holo, and alternate art variants separately
    - Track your inventory with quantity information
    - Add notes, categorize your cards

    ❖ SET TRACKING

    - See how many cards you have from each set in real time
    - Track completion percentage
    - List your missing cards, create a wishlist
    - Be the first to discover new sets

    ❖ COLLECTION VALUE

    - See the total value of your collection in real time
    - Track the current value of individual cards
    - Monitor value changes over time with graphs
    - Highlight your most valuable cards

    ❖ SUPPORTED GAMES

    - Pokemon TCG — All modern sets and classic collections
    - One Piece Card Game — Complete OP sets
    - Riftbound — Riot Games' new TCG is supported

    More games coming soon!

    ❖ QUICK AND EASY REGISTRATION

    - Sign in with Google — create your account in one click

    - Classic registration option with email

    ❖ ACCESS EVERYWHERE

    Your collection is securely stored in the cloud. From phone to tablet, from tablet to web — your collection is with you no matter which device you access from.

    ❖ PRIVACY & SECURITY

    Your data is protected in accordance with GDPR regulations. Your collection information is only yours and will not be shared with anyone unless you give permission.

    ❖ UPCOMING FEATURES

    - Card buying and selling marketplace
    - Trading with other collectors
    - Community binder sharing
    - More TCG support

    For questions, suggestions and feedback:
