    Official description

    SpecSnap — Snap it. Match any spec.

    Create perfect passport and ID photos in minutes, right from your phone. SpecSnap ships with 46 government photo specs across 20 countries and regions — Singapore, Malaysia, Indonesia, Thailand, the Philippines, the United States, the Schengen area, Australia, India, the United Kingdom, Mexico, China, Saudi Arabia, the United Arab Emirates, Canada, Japan, Hong Kong, Taiwan, New Zealand, and Vietnam. No more expensive photo studios or rejected applications.

    SUPPORTED DOCUMENTS
    - Passport photos
    - Visa photos (incl. US DS-160 and Schengen short-stay)
    - National ID / IC photos (MyKad, KTP, Philippine ID, Thai ID, Singapore IC)
    - Work passes, employment passes, driving licences, and country-specific permits
    - Country-specific size and format compliance

    OFFICIAL SOURCES & DISCLAIMER
    SpecSnap is an independent app and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or acting on behalf of any government agency or authority. Official photo-requirement sources for every supported country are listed at https://specsnap.app/sources — always confirm current requirements with the official government source before you submit.

    PRIVATE BY DESIGN — NO CLOUD UPLOADS
    Your photos never leave your phone. Unlike most AI photo apps that send your face to remote servers for processing, SpecSnap processes photos locally using on-device ML. No account required. No photos stored on our servers. Works offline.

    AI-POWERED PHOTO PROCESSING
    - Automatic background removal — instantly replaces any background with the required white or blue backdrop
    - Smart face detection — automatically locates your face and checks positioning
    - Real-time compliance feedback — guides you to meet official requirements for head size, eye position, and margins
    - Before-and-after comparison — see exactly what changed

    EASY CROP AND ADJUST
    - Drag-to-adjust crop tool with live compliance indicators
    - Visual guides show you when head size, centering, and spacing meet official standards
    - No guesswork — green means you are good to go

    PRINT-READY LAYOUTS
    - Generate print layouts for standard 4R and 6R photo paper
    - Perfectly spaced grids ready for home printing or your local print shop
    - Save time and money — print as many copies as you need
