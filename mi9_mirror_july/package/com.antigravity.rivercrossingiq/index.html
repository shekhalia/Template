    Official description

    Welcome to River Crossing IQ, the ultimate brain-teasing logic puzzle game that will put your critical thinking and problem-solving skills to the true test!

    Based on the world-famous river crossing riddles, this game brings you 100 uniquely crafted and highly challenging levels. Your mission is simple yet incredibly tricky: safely transport all characters from the left bank of the river to the right bank using a small boat.

    🧠 How to Play & The Challenge:
    You must follow strict logical rules. Certain characters cannot be left alone together on the riverbank! For example, if you leave the wolf alone with the sheep, the sheep will be eaten. If the thief is left without the guard, the treasure will be stolen. You must find the exact, safe sequence of moves to solve each puzzle!

    🌟 Key Features:

    100 Mind-Bending Levels: Ranging from easy warm-ups to "Very Hard" logic puzzles that will melt your brain.

    Unique Scenarios: Play classic riddles (Farmer, Wolf, Goat) along with creative new themes like Art Gallery Heists, Safari Parks, Alien Encounters, and Ninja Battles.

    Smart Hint System: Stuck on an impossible level? Don't worry! Simply use the 'Hint' button, watch a short video, and the game will reveal the next correct safe moves to guide you forward.

    Multi-Language Support: Play comfortably in English, Hindi, or Gujarati.

    Stunning Graphics: Enjoy high-quality, realistic character designs and a smooth, interactive game board.

    Brain Training: Perfect for kids and adults looking to improve their IQ, memory, and logical reasoning.

    Can you master all 100 levels and prove your genius? There is only one way to find out.
