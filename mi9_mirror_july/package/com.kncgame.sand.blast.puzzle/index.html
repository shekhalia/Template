    Official description

    KEEP THE SAND FLOWING. BEAT THE JAM. REVEAL THE ART.

    Sandoa Jam: Sand Flow Puzzle is a colorful sand flow puzzle where timing, color matching, and available space shape every move. Guide moving buckets along the conveyor, collect sand of the matching color, and fill each section to reveal vibrant sand art.

    ▶ HOW TO PLAY
    • Tap to place and guide color buckets onto the conveyor.
    • Match each bucket with sand of the same color.
    • Keep open slots for the buckets that come next.
    • Choose the right order to keep the conveyor from getting jammed.
    • Fill every color section to complete the picture.
    • Adapt your strategy as new layouts and obstacles make each puzzle more challenging.

    ◆ KEEP THE FLOW UNDER CONTROL
    In Sandoa Jam, every bucket changes the flow. A smart move keeps the conveyor moving, while a poor choice can block the next bucket and leave you with no free space.

    Look ahead, manage your open slots, and decide which color should move first. Each sand flow puzzle rewards careful timing, the right order, and smart planning. Complete the color-matching challenge, keep the sand moving, and enjoy the satisfying art reveal at the end of every level.

    ◆ WHY YOU’LL ENJOY SANDOA JAM
    • Simple tap controls that are easy to learn.
    • Color bucket puzzles with smooth, satisfying sand flow.
    • Conveyor challenges that reward smart decisions.
    • Vibrant sand art revealed section by section.
    • Short levels that are quick to start and rewarding to complete.
    • New layouts and obstacles that create fresh strategic choices.

    Can you guide every bucket, avoid the jam, and reveal the complete sand artwork?
