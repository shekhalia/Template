    Official description

    A unique puzzle game where numbers create the path.

    Merge numbers to solve challenging puzzles and reveal hidden images.
    What looks simple at first becomes a deep and addictive brain challenge.

    🧩 HOW IT WORKS
    Drag across tiles and combine numbers.
    The sum creates the next number – and it works in reverse too.
    Fill the entire board with no gaps to complete the puzzle.

    🖼️ GALLERY MODE
    Solve puzzles to uncover hidden images piece by piece.
    Each completed puzzle reveals more of the picture.
    Unlock new images and progress through levels.

    ⏱️ SURVIVAL MODE
    Play against time and test your skills.
    Solve puzzles to gain extra time and reach higher levels.
    Compete on the leaderboard and track your progress.

    🧘 RELAX MODE
    No timer. No pressure.

    Play any puzzle size at your own pace.
    Think freely, solve calmly, and enjoy stress-free gameplay.
    Perfect for relaxing while still challenging your brain.

    🎁 FEATURES
    • Unique and original puzzle mechanics
    • Math-based logic gameplay
    • Multiple board sizes (3x3 to 8x12)
    • Relaxing yet challenging experience
    • Rewards, boosters, and surprises
    • Leaderboards and progression system

    If you enjoy puzzle, math, and brain-training games, this is a completely new experience.
