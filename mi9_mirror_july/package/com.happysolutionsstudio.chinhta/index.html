    Official description

    Through simple, engaging games reminiscent of "school days" with blackboards and chalk, the Spelling Prodigy app aims to provide not only fun entertainment but also a contribution to preserving the purity of the Vietnamese language.

    - Five obstacle courses to test your spelling skills.
    - A diverse vocabulary for both spoken and written language, realistic and increasing with each level.
    - Diverse and easy-to-use challenging activities such as swiping left and right, choosing the correct word.
    - A "Spelling Master Corner" to share your spelling master titles with friends.

    Test your spelling skills:

    - Complete the questions correctly within the time limit and collect enough EXP for each level to become the owner of rewards and title medals at the end of each level.

    - Refer to the "Instructions" section on the homepage for detailed instructions.
