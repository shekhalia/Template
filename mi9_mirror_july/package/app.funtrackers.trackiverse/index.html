    Official description

    TRACKIVERSE: YOUR MODULAR MULTIVERSE

    CURRENT MODULES:
    Biermass-Tracker: Because Only a Full Liter Counts.
    Bring the authentic Bavarian lifestyle straight to your iPhone. Track your beer at the beer garden, pub, or festival using our unique "Full-Liter Logic". Your stats will only credit you with a traditional "Mass" once you have actually reached a full liter (including a clever buffer for 3x 0.33l bottles). Features an animated beer mug, location logging, and your personal "Schee" archive.

    Klimmzug Tracker: Your Vertical Legend.
    Pull-ups (Klimmzüge)) are a pure battle against gravity. Document your reps, track your added weight, and optimize your workout with the integrated rest timer. Keep an eye on your total strength performance – from individual sessions to your lifetime total weight lifted.

    Espresso-Tracker: From Daily Grind to Mindful Sip.
    An espresso is more than just caffeine – it’s arguably the smallest form of "me-time." Document your mindful breaks and keep track of your daily rituals. Use the smart sharing feature to easily send your consumption to the office coffee fund via WhatsApp or email.

    Eiskugel-Tracker (Scoop Tracker): Your Summer, Scoop by Scoop.
    Enjoyment isn't a statistic – it’s fun! Whether it's a classic cone or a quick cup: this is your digital diary for the year's sweetest moments. Document every scoop, mark the location, and create your personal map of happy memories.

    Bergschein-Tracker (Festival Challenge): Your 12-Day Journey to Legend.
    At the 271st Erlangen Bergkirchweih 2026, watch a rumor become your personal myth. Document your progress from the first stamp to the final "Berg" reality. Every stamp is a step toward making the legend real.

    Lesen-Tracker (Reading Tracker): Your Digital Bookshelf.
    Scan books via ISBN, upload your own covers, and log reading time. Use the built-in stopwatch to break progress into precise sessions and stay in tune with your reading pace.

    Trinkgeld-Tracker (Tip Tracker): From Spare Change to a Big Goal.
    Tips are honest appreciation, but they often vanish in daily life. This module makes your hard-earned extras visible. Log your daily successes in seconds and watch small change turn into big dreams.

    Kennzeichen-Tracker (License Plate Hunter): The Ultimate Road Trip Hunt.
    Can you spot all 714 German regional license plates? Log your sightings and see the distance to the plate's origin on a map. Turn every drive into a challenge! (Note: For passengers only!)

    Puzzle-Tracker: From the First Corner to the Final Piece.
    Document your masterpieces and measure progress. Use the stopwatch for your sessions and create a photo series to watch your puzzle grow piece by piece.

    Pfand-Tracker (Recycling Tracker): Do Good and Make It Visible.
    Track the bottles and cans you’ve collected from nature or the city. Follow your contribution to a cleaner environment and see exactly how your effort pays off in real money.

    WHY TRACKIVERSE?
    MODULAR: Use only the trackers you actually need.
    LOCAL: Your data stays on your device.
    TRANSPARENT: You decide through your personal settings.
    FAST: No account, no login – start tracking immediately.

    No matter what you want to measure, collect, or document – welcome to your Trackiverse.
