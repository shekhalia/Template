    RAID: Shadow Legends Overview

    RAID: Shadow Legends offers a detailed turn based RPG loop centered on collecting and building a roster of champions. Players spend time managing resources, completing daily objectives, and strategizing team compositions for battles against AI controlled bosses and in the multiplayer Arena.

    Common praise highlights the satisfying depth of character progression and the volume of available content, with many finding it engaging for daily sessions. Frequent criticisms point to a demanding champion acquisition system and persistent promotional pop ups. Technical issues like freezing are also noted by some.

    This title suits players who enjoy tactical, incremental progression and don't mind a grind oriented structure. Key aspects include a large champion pool, artifact equipment systems, faction based strategy, and regular collaborative events. For those seeking a long term collection and strategy project, RAID: Shadow Legends provides a substantial framework.
