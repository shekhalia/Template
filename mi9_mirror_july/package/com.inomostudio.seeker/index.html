    Official description

    Take a quick chakra test, discover what feels blocked, and get a personalized Daily Mission to help you feel more grounded, clear, and aligned.

    Seeker helps you understand your energy and turn that insight into a daily practice you can actually follow.

    TAKE YOUR CHAKRA ANALYSIS
    Complete a short guided check-in based on mood, reflection, and self-awareness. Seeker gives you a personalized chakra result to help you understand what feels blocked, overactive, or balanced.

    GET YOUR DAILY MISSION
    Your result becomes your Daily Mission — a focused next step designed to support you with personalized practices throughout the day.

    TURN INSIGHT INTO PRACTICE
    Explore guided meditations, affirmations, ambiance sessions, and courses tailored to your current state. Choose the session length, voice, and sound settings that fit your moment, then save the sessions you want to return to.

    FRESH CONTENT, REGULARLY
    Fresh affirmations and guided content are added regularly, so you always have new ways to reconnect, reset, and realign.

    WHAT YOU’LL FIND INSIDE
    • Chakra Analysis
    • Personalized chakra results
    • Daily Mission recommendations
    • Guided meditations
    • Affirmations
    • Ambiance and sound-based sessions
    • Courses and curated journeys
    • Saved and recent sessions

    PREMIUM & SUBSCRIPTIONS
    Seeker offers auto-renewing subscriptions that unlock the full experience, including premium content, longer sessions, and additional voices and sounds.
    Manage or cancel your subscription in your App Store account settings. Any unused portion of a free trial will be forfeited when you purchase a subscription.

    DISCLAIMER
    Seeker is for wellness, mindfulness, and self-reflection. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.
