    Official description

    i-SHELF is a digital publishing and book marketplace platform that enables authors, researchers, and readers to publish, access, and purchase books in one place.

    Authors can upload manuscripts, research materials, and books for review and publication. Each submission is verified before being published to ensure quality, authenticity, and compliance with publishing standards.

    Readers can explore a growing library of books across different categories, securely purchase content, and access their library anytime.

    Key features:

    * Author book upload and management
    * Admin verification (ISBN/ISSN validation)
    * Secure book purchase and access system
    * Digital library for readers
    * Content discovery and organization tools

    i-SHELF provides a structured digital environment for publishing and commerce, connecting authors and readers through a secure and organized platform.
