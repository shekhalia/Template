    Official description

    Step into a medieval 3D puzzle game where every level is a race to spot the right match.

    Explore a castle world filled with polished items, hidden pieces, and clever match 3 challenges. Spin the carousel, search the board, and connect matching items before time runs out. Each puzzle is simple to start and satisfying to master, with timed play, power ups, bonuses, achievements, leaderboards, and online and offline play built in.

    A 3D MATCH GAME WITH A MEDIEVAL TWIST

    This is a match 3 puzzle adventure set inside a royal medieval world. You will search through detailed 3D items inspired by castles, markets, knights, treasure rooms, potion tables, and enchanted halls. The goal is clear. Find the matching items, make the match, and clear the level.

    The challenge comes from how fast you can notice what belongs together. Some items are easy to spot. Others are tucked behind the carousel or mixed into the scene. Every level asks you to look closer, move faster, and make smart choices before the timer ends.

    SPIN THE CAROUSEL

    The spinning carousel gives every puzzle a fresh feel. Rotate the view to discover hidden items, plan your next match, and keep the board moving. The 3D layout makes each level feel hands-on without getting complicated. You can play for a few minutes or settle in for a longer run.

    TIMED PLAY THAT KEEPS THINGS MOVING

    Every puzzle has a timer, so each match matters. Beat the clock to complete levels, earn bonuses, and push your score higher. Timed play adds focus without making the game feel heavy. It is quick, clean, and easy to come back to.

    POWER UPS WHEN YOU NEED A SAVE

    Use power ups to get through tough moments. Find an item faster, shuffle the board, clear space, or give yourself the extra help needed to finish the puzzle. Choosing when to use a power up is part of the strategy. Save it too long and you may run out of time. Use it well and the win feels even better.

    BONUSES, ACHIEVEMENTS, AND LEADERBOARDS

    Keep playing to collect bonuses and unlock achievements across the kingdom. Chase better clears, improve your time, and compete on leaderboards when you play online. There is always another puzzle to finish and another goal to beat.

    PLAY ONLINE OR OFFLINE

    Play online to sync progress, compete on leaderboards, and keep achievements updated. Play offline whenever you want to enjoy a puzzle without a connection. It is perfect for travel, quick breaks, waiting rooms, flights, and quiet downtime.

    WHY YOU WILL LIKE IT

    Medieval 3D match 3 puzzles with a clean, polished look.

    A spinning carousel that makes every level feel active.

    Timed play that rewards sharp eyes and quick decisions.

    Power ups that help you clear difficult puzzles.

    Bonuses, achievements, and leaderboards for extra goals.

    Online and offline play, so the game is ready anywhere.

    Simple matching rules with real challenge as levels grow.

    A MEDIEVAL PUZZLE GAME FOR QUICK BREAKS

    Whether you play one level or ten, each puzzle gives you a new set of items to search, match, and clear. The medieval theme keeps the world charming, the 3D items keep the board interesting, and the timed play keeps every move meaningful.

    Start your next match, spin the carousel, and see how far you can climb.
