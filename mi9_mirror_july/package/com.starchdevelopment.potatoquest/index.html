    Official description

    Not all potatoes are sprouted equal. Not all dreams are destined to be fulfilled. But what of you? Is your name fated to be forgotten? Or will you traverse the inexplicably levitated
    platforms, across the starry night skies and into legend?
    Welcome to Potato Quest! Join our hero Spud on his heartwarming journey to becoming a better yam, and also to get a really good score. Will you be crown potato royalty?
    Potato Quest is a one-thumb endless platformer. Pull back and release to launch your potato across an endless run of platforms - the further you drag,
    the bigger the leap. Every gap you clear stacks your score.
    - Solo Quest: an endless run to beat your personal best.
    - Bracket Battle: race a ghost replay of a real player near your skill tier and earn coins for the win. Climb seven bracket tiers from Spud Sprout to Spud Royale.
    - Daily Duel: everyone plays the same seeded course each day. Top the leaderboard to be crowned the day's champion.
    - Global leaderboards, daily crowns, and a stats and a stats page that tracks your run.
    - Unlock potato skins, parallax backgrounds, and hats with coins and high-score milestones.
    Sign in with Apple or Google to carry your coins, crowns, outfits, and high scores across devices. Play offline; your solo scores sync when you reconnect.
