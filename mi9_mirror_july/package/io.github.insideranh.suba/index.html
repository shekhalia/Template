    Official description

    Suba is the most complete, stable, and secure POS and business management system for Peru. Control sales, inventory, and finances from your cell phone or computer without complications.

    WHAT MAKES SUBA DIFFERENT FROM OTHER APPS?

    • 100% Offline Mode: Keep selling without internet. The app saves your transactions locally and automatically syncs them when the signal returns.

    • Zero-Knowledge Privacy: End-to-end encryption. Your data is yours; not even our servers can see your customers' data.

    ADVANCED INVENTORY AND SALES FEATURES:

    • Point of Sale (POS): Record sales instantly by searching by name or scanning barcodes with your camera. Accept cash, cards, transfers, and credit. Issue digital receipts with QR codes instantly.

    • Smart Product Suggestions: Fast predictive system. Type "Ca" and the app suggests "Coffee" based on your catalog to help you sell faster.

    • Bulletproof Inventory: Know your real-time stock, manage batches, expiration alerts, and multiple prices (list, sale, wholesale). Allows for bulk batch changes.

    • Clothing Store Variants: Automatic system for generating sizes, colors, and styles to organize garments without duplicating products.

    • Barcode Printing: Generate and print barcodes directly from the system to label your merchandise.

    • Purchase Orders and Suppliers: Organize your supplies and maintain a detailed change history to audit every transaction.

    • Returns and Recycling: Manage customer returns efficiently. If you accidentally delete a product or sale, the archiving system lets you restore it with one click.

    ONLINE STORE, RESTAURANTS, AND TEAM:

    • Your Automatic Online Store: Public web catalog with your personalized link to receive orders directly to your WhatsApp, with real-time tracking and email notifications. Integrate your social media accounts like Instagram and TikTok.

    • Restaurants and Production: Visual map of your tables with real-time status updates. Create recipes where ingredient stock is deducted only upon sale, create combo meals, and manage manufacturing orders.

    • Team and Branch Management: Invite your employees via email and assign them specific roles (Cashier, Server, Inventory Clerk). Monitor the audit history to know exactly who created, edited, or deleted something.

    • Cash Register and Accounts Receivable: Open and close the daily cash register with automatic reconciliation. Clean sales/expense charts and credit management with automatic payment reminders via push notifications or email.

    MORE BENEFITS:

    ✔ Web panel for desktop and Android mobile app synchronized in real time.

    ✔ Your data is 100% yours: Export it in CSV, JSON, or PDF format whenever you need it.

    ✔ No binding contracts or surprises.

    ✔ System built by listening to the suggestions of our user community.

    Ideal for: Bodegas, minimarkets, restaurants, hardware stores, clothing stores, bazaars, and any business ready to grow.
