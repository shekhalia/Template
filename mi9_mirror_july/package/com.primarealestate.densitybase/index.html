    Official description

    Tap. Time it. Drop. Jokers Jewels stacks a swaying gem tile onto the tower below at every tap, and every landing decides whether the next tile widens or shrinks. Miss the alignment and the tile clips down to only the overlap — miss too much and the tower closes. Land dead center and the tile widens back to full width, keeping the run alive. Jokers Jewels is a pocket arcade about steady thumbs and quiet focus.

    💎 The stack

    The swinging tile sways across the screen, faster with every floor, and a single tap decides its final position. Perfect drops widen the plate; near-misses shave it thin; a bad drop ends the run. Every floor is a separate decision. Every run is a separate story. There is no random spin, no reel, no auto-play — just the tile, the timing, and the count of the best floor. Beginners reach floor ten in a few tries. Reaching floor thirty takes real practice.

    ⏱ Three ways to play

    Easy mode keeps the swing slow and the tolerance forgiving — good for warming up the reflex. Normal is the default balance. Hard cuts the tile smaller from the first floor and speeds up the swing per floor, so combos become the only way to survive past twenty. All three modes save separately in the ledger. Personal best per mode is possible. Switch mode from the home selector before starting a run.

    Daily tower

    Every day Jokers Jewels seeds one shared tower for every player — the same swing speed, the same starting width, the same color sequence. One attempt per day. Compare a score with a friend, retry tomorrow. A streak counter tracks how many days in a row a daily run was completed. Skip a day and the streak resets, but the daily tower resets every midnight in local time.

    Ledger and stats

    The stats page keeps the best floor across all runs, the best combo streak, the total perfect drops, and how many days in a row a session happened. There is a per-mode breakdown and a lifetime tally. Nothing leaves the device — all counters are stored locally on the phone, and there is no account to sign in to.

    Visual style

    The palette is midnight blue, emerald, and copper — matte gem colors on a dim backdrop, easy on the eyes for evening sessions. Every tile draws with a faceted highlight and a soft drop shadow, and perfect drops flash briefly to reward the timing.

    Tips for higher floors

    Watch the tile enter the frame before tapping; the swing starts fast on Hard. Line up the shadow with the plate below, not the tile itself. Combos are the only way to keep width past floor twenty, so aim for perfect drops early. When the plate gets narrow, small taps at the swing turning point often work better than trying to time the middle.

    🚫 What Jokers Jewels is not

    Jokers Jewels here is the name of a tap-to-drop arcade. It is not a casino product. It is not a slot game. There are no reels, no spin buttons, no wagering, no in-game currency, no purchase gates, no chip counters. If jokers jewels casino was the search — this is a different app that happens to share the phrase in its title. The same goes for Joker's Jewels and Joker Jewels — those are slot titles from other publishers, and this app has none of that content. The gameplay is a tower stacker, and everything runs offline once installed.

    Compatibility

    Runs on Android phones and tablets. No account, no sign-in, no login. Works fully offline after the first launch. Small on storage, low on battery, no background service, no data collection.

    Closing

    Jokers Jewels is for a short focused sitting between other things — a metro ride, a coffee refill, a break. Enough depth to chase a real personal best, small enough to close and forget until tomorrow's daily run.
