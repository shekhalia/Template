    Official description

    Plan your perfect trip - in minutes.

    Viala is an AI-powered travel planner that builds your ideal itinerary through a simple, swipe-based experience. Tell Viala where you want to go. Swipe through AI-curated activities. Get a complete day-by-day itinerary. Done.

    How it works

    → Answer a few quick questions about your trip
    → Swipe through AI-curated activities, restaurants, and experiences
    → Watch your day-by-day itinerary build in real time
    → Adjust, reorder, and share when you're ready

    No more hours lost in travel blogs, review sites, or spreadsheets. Viala turns trip planning into something you actually enjoy.

    FEATURES

    🗺 AI Trip Planning
    Get a complete, day-by-day itinerary in under a minute. Activities, restaurants, transit times - all organized automatically.

    ⚡ Quick Plan
    Open the app anywhere and get instant local suggestions based on your current location. Perfect for spontaneous days out.

    🌤 Weather-Aware Itineraries
    See weather forecasts built directly into your trip timeline so you can plan around the conditions.

    🏨 Stay Suggestions
    Get AI-ranked accommodation recommendations that match your destination and budget.

    👥 Trip Sharing & Collaboration
    Share your itinerary with friends or travel companions. Plan together, explore together.

    🔁 Import & Export
    Share trips via QR code or import ID. Every shared trip is one tap away from becoming yours.
    💰 Budget Overview
    Set a budget and see how your planned activities stack up against it.

    FREE TO USE

    The full trip planning experience - swiping, itinerary generation, sharing - is free. No paywalls on the core features.

    ABOUT AI-GENERATED CONTENT

    Viala uses AI to generate travel suggestions. Content may be inaccurate, outdated, or incomplete. Always verify opening hours, prices, visa requirements, and local conditions before you travel. Viala is a planning and information tool - it is not a travel agent, tour operator, or booking platform.
