    Marvel Contest of Champions Overview

    Marvel Contest of Champions centers on one on one combat, where players swipe and tap to chain combos and activate special moves. The core loop involves collecting characters, leveling them up, and navigating a lengthy story mode and monthly events.

    Long term players often praise the steady content updates and roster of characters. Common criticisms focus on the complex upgrade systems requiring multiple resources and a reliance on random chance for obtaining new fighters. Some later modes are noted for their high difficulty.

    This title suits players who enjoy collecting characters and don't mind a gradual, long term progression system. The gameplay of Marvel Contest of Champions is straightforward, but advancement requires consistent engagement.
