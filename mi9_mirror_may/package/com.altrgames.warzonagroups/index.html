    Official description

    War Zona Groups — a real-time strategy game set in a near future where the world has changed forever.

    After a global nuclear catastrophe, vast territories turned into deadly Anomalous Zones. Radiation storms rage across the wastelands, mutants roam abandoned cities, and scattered factions fight for control over the most dangerous and valuable lands on Earth.

    You are the leader of your own group.

    ⚠ Build and upgrade your base in a hostile post-apocalyptic world
    ⚔ Command fighters, raiders, and elite units in real-time battles
    ☢ Capture Anomalous Zones to gain rare resources and strategic advantage
    🧬 Fight mutants, military remnants, and rival factions
    🔧 Research technologies and strengthen your war machine

    In this world, there are no heroes — only survivors.

    Every zone holds power. Every faction wants control.
    Military forces try to restore order. Raiders seek chaos and profit. Independent groups fight for survival. Mutants defend what radiation created.

    Choose your strategy:
    – Aggressive expansion
    – Defensive fortification
    – Tactical resource control
    – Domination through technology

    The future belongs to those who are ready to take it.
