    Official description

    The sweetest cookie ever baked is here 🍰 Who could resist?
    and it’s only in Cookie Sorting, the tastiest puzzle game!

    Cookie Sorting is a fresh twist on sorting games. It’s neither match 3 nor match 6 - it’s match 4 cookies with addictive color-sorting gameplay. Step into a sweet kitchen full of cakes, pies, and yummy desserts waiting to be organized. As the cookie chef, your goal is simple: sort and merge cookies until the tray is complete.

    🍰 HOW TO PLAY 🍰
    Tap and move cookies into the right tray.
    Match 4 identical cookies to clear the tray.
    Plan your moves carefully - don’t run out of space!
    Unlock new cookies, cakes, and pies as you progress.
    Collect stars and bonuses to keep playing.

    🥧 FEATURES 🥧
    🧠 Addictive brain games with fun and strategic merge gameplay
    🍓 40++ delicious cookies in the collection: Mousse, Strawberry shortcake, Watermelon chiffon, red velvet, donuts, …
    🎨 Bright color sorting visuals with smooth animations.
    🎵 Relaxing games vibe with satisfying sound effects.
    🎁 Spin the lucky wheel and earn great rewards.
    🌟 Free & easy to play with endless levels of challenge.
