    Official description

    Ancient Tiles: Match3 Journey Through History

    Embark on a cinematic puzzle adventure that spans thousands of years. From the golden sands of Ancient Egypt to the misty peaks of feudal Japan, the neon glow of the Modern age, and even the mysterious landscapes of an Alien world — match your way through history across 10 stunningly themed civilizations.

    Whether you are looking for a relaxing zen quest or a challenging mystery to solve, Ancient Tiles offers a unique tile match experience that blends history and heritage into every level.

    Four Unique Game Modes:

    🔲 Grid Match: Experience classic match3 puzzles with objective-based goals, cascading matches, and explosive power-ups. It is the ultimate matchthree challenge for puzzle lovers.

    🃏 Deck Mode: Tap tiles from the central pile into your deck and clear matches before it fills up. Simple to learn, satisfying to master—a true antique style puzzle.

    🎯 Shooter Mode: Fire colored tiles from your launcher to clear your goals before you run out of time. Fast-paced, strategic, and endlessly replayable.

    🧠 Memo Match: Sharpen your mind with this memory-based challenge. Reveal hidden tiles and find their matches before the timer runs out. A soothing yet sharp brain trainer.

    Key Features:

    🎨 Stunning Digital Art: Premium cinematic backgrounds bursting with atmospheric detail — god rays, volumetric fog, and historically accurate environments that celebrate world heritage.

    🏛️ 10 Epic Civilizations: Take a journey through Egypt, Greece, Rome, Japan, the Aztecs, China, the Vikings, Africa, the Modern age, and otherworldly Alien realms. Every level is a riddle waiting to be solved.

    🤖 Loyal Robot Companions: Deploy your AttackRobot to blast through tiles or call upon the HintRobot to highlight your next move. Equip powerful relics and badges — Maintenance, Fast Forward, Additional Star, and more — to conquer the hardest levels.

    ⚡ Power-Ups & Strategy: Use your braintraining skills with Bomb Barrages, Row Destroys, Magic Matches, and Deck Reverts to give you the edge when the puzzle gets tough.

    🏆 Daily Events: Take on special challenge levels with unique difficulty tiers and earn exclusive rewards for your civilization.

    Become the ultimate voyager and master the Ancient Tiles!
