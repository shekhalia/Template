    Official description

    Ahia Main Market is a Global Premier E-commerce marketplace connecting buyers, sellers, and delivery agents in one seamless platform.

    FOR BUYERS:
    Browse thousands of products from verified Global sellers. Enjoy secure escrow-protected payments, real-time price negotiation, and AI-powered product recommendations. Track your deliveries in real-time and communicate directly with sellers.

    FOR SELLERS:
    Set up your store in minutes. Manage products, track orders, and receive payments securely. Our AI assistant helps you optimize listings and reach more customers. Enjoy fraud protection with our comprehensive KYC verification system.

    FOR DELIVERY AGENTS:
    Accept delivery jobs, navigate with built-in maps, and earn money on your schedule. Our smart routing system helps you maximize deliveries.

    KEY FEATURES:
    • Secure Escrow Payments - Your money is protected until delivery is confirmed
    • AI-Powered Shopping - Smart recommendations and search
    • Real-Time Chat & Calls - Communicate directly with buyers and sellers
    • Price Negotiation - Make offers and negotiate the best deals
    • Multi-Currency Support - Shop in NGN, GHS, KES, ZAR, and more
    • KYC Verification - Verified sellers you can trust
    • Real-Time Delivery Tracking - Know exactly where your order is
    • Crypto Payments - Pay with cryptocurrency for added flexibility

    Built for Everyone. Join thousands of users already trading on Ahia Main Market!
