    Official description

    SBBS School is the official app of Salomon Betsaleel Business School, a business school based in Abidjan, Ivory Coast.

    It allows SBBS leaders to track their training in a structured and secure way.

    MAIN FEATURES:

    - Attendance and attendance tracking
    - Access to courses, assessments, and deliverables
    - Messaging with coaches and management
    - Personal investment plan tracking
    - Generation of certificates and PDF documents
    - Alumni area for certified former leaders
    - Training notifications and calendar

    SECURE ACCESS:
    Access to the app is reserved for leaders enrolled at SBBS School. Accounts are created by the administration. Public registration is not available.

    SBBS SCHOOL:
    Salomon Betsaleel Business School trains entrepreneurial leaders in Ivory Coast and other French-speaking African countries.
