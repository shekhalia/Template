    🤖 Meet KIM — Your AI Task Companion

    Magik Workflow isn't just another to-do app. KIM, your intelligent voice
    companion, greets you every morning, alerts you to critical tasks, and

    ✨ WHAT MAKES MAGIK WORKFLOW DIFFERENT
    • KIM speaks to you — morning motivation, evening wrap-up, productivity
    tips
    • KIM reacts when you complete, add or delete a task
    • Voice commands to create tasks hands-free
    • Ambient lo-fi music to keep you in the zone

    📋 SMART TASK MANAGEMENT
    • Kanban board: To Do → In Progress → Done
    • 4 priority levels: Critical, High, Medium, Low
    • 6 categories: Work, Personal, Health, Learning, Family, Finance
    • Automatic emoji suggestion for each task
    • Daily streak & completed task counter

    🎯 STAY FOCUSED
    • Eisenhower matrix view (urgent vs. important)
    • KIM alerts you when too many tasks are in progress
    • Daily performance summary

    🆓 FREE VERSION INCLUDES
    • 1 task list
    • Up to 10 active tasks
    • Full KIM companion experience
