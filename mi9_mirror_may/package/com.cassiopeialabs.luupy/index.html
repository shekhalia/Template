    Official description

    Luupy is the easiest subscription manager so you're never surprised by a charge on your account.

    DOES THIS SOUND FAMILIAR?

    • You've forgotten how many subscriptions you have active.

    • You've been charged for a free trial you wanted to cancel.

    • You don't know how much you spend each month on Netflix, Spotify, gym memberships, and mortgage.

    • You want a clear schedule of your recurring payments.

    WHAT LUUPY DOES:

    • Add all your subscriptions and recurring payments: streaming services, gym memberships, mortgage, rent, insurance, tutoring, childcare, or any monthly bill.

    • Recognizable icons from hundreds of brands (Netflix, Spotify, Disney+, HBO, Amazon Prime, Apple TV+, YouTube Premium, etc.).

    • Reminders before each charge so you can decide whether to renew or cancel in time.

    • Monthly and annual totals of what you spend on subscriptions.

    • Clear statistics by category: entertainment, finance,

    health, transportation, home, education, and more.

    • Integrated calendar to see at a glance what's charged each day.

    • Light mode and dark mode.

    • Home screen widget with your next payment.

    • Support for medication reminders as if they were another

    subscription.

    LUUPY PREMIUM:

    Unlock advanced features:

    • Unlimited subscriptions.

    • Premium dark mode.

    • Cloud sync across all your devices.

    • Priority access to new features.

    Price: €5.99/month or €1.99/week.

    You can cancel anytime from your
    Google Play account settings. The subscription automatically renews
    unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the period.

    PRIVACY FIRST:

    • Works without an account if you want: Luupy saves everything locally
    on your device.

    • If you sign in with Google, Apple, or email, your data is securely synced with Firebase.

    • We never sell your data to third parties.

    • You can delete your account and all your data at any time from the app settings.

    Stop checking bank statements to find charges you forgot. Luupy does it for you.
