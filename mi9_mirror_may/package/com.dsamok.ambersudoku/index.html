    Official description

    🌟 Introduction
    Amber Sudoku is a polished logic puzzle app built for players who want more than one kind of Sudoku. Solve classic grids, explore clever variants, customize the way the board looks, and settle into a clean puzzle experience designed for daily play.

    🧩 Play Five Puzzle Styles in One App
    🔢 Classic Sudoku: The timeless 9x9 number puzzle.

    🧮 Killer Sudoku / Caged Sudoku: Solve cages where each group must add up to its target total.

    📐 Futoshiki: Use greater-than and less-than clues to complete each row and column.

    📝 Kakuro: Fill number-crossword runs so every clue sum works.

    🌡️ Thermo Sudoku: Follow thermometer paths where values increase from bulb to tip.

    🧠 Built for Focused Solving
    ✏️ Use Pen and Notes modes for clean final answers and pencil-mark planning.

    💡 Tap Hint when you need a nudge.

    ↩️ Undo moves and erase entries as you refine your solve.

    ✅ Smart validation helps spot conflicts without breaking your flow.

    🏆 Choose Easy, Medium, Hard, or Expert difficulty.

    🎨 Make the Board Your Own
    🔠 Play with classic numbers or themed symbol sets.

    🔓 Unlock premium themes including Weather, Music, Animals, and Travel.

    🌗 Switch between light and dark visual styles.

    ✨ Enjoy animated backgrounds, refined sounds, and haptic feedback.

    ⚙️ Play Your Way
    ⏳ Zen Time lets you set a focused countdown session.

    ⏰ Interval alarms help you stay aware of time during longer puzzles.

    📅 Daily reminders can bring you back for a quick brain workout.

    📊 Track your puzzle history, streak, coins, and progress.

    🎮 Sign in with Google Play Games to sync supported progress and purchases.

    💎 Amber Premium
    🚫 Remove interstitial ads.

    ♾️ Get unlimited, ad-free play.

    🖼️ Access premium visual themes.

    🕊️ Enjoy a smoother experience with fewer interruptions.

    🚀 Conclusion
    Whether you are a Sudoku beginner, a daily puzzle solver, or a logic fan looking for Kakuro, Futoshiki, Killer, and Thermo challenges in one place, Amber Sudoku gives you a calm, colorful space to train your mind.
