    Official description

    MisterX is the party game for evenings with friends, family rounds, and spontaneous groups. You only need one phone and you can jump right in. Every player gets a hidden role: most players see a secret word, but the MisterX player gets no word and has to bluff without getting caught.

    Here is how it works: The Imposter knows nothing and still talks along. The others know everything and do their best not to give it away. Somewhere in between, there is bluffing, laughing, guessing, and unmasking. No match is like another and every round brings new surprises.

    What makes MisterX stand out:
    - One smartphone for everyone: ideal for trips, parties, and travel
    - Multiplayer for up to 30 players
    - Exclusive 2-player mode - great fun even with just two people
    - Detailed and clear tutorial - start right away, no confusion
    - 12 supported languages
    - Timer freely adjustable, from fast to tactical rounds
    - Personalize players: freely choose names and icons
    - Adjust colors and look to match your style
    - Unlock achievements and discover a hidden secret
    - Custom categories for insider terms and your own themes
    - Game history and useful stats always in view

    Whether it is a casual round or a hard-core bluff battle, MisterX is easy to learn, quick to start, and always different. Start your next round now and find out who the Imposter is!
