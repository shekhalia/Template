    Official description

    Actor life simulation game.

    Build your Hollywood career from unknown extra to A-list star. Audition for roles, film movies, and watch them climb the box office in the deepest offline celebrity life sim on mobile.

    ★ BUILD A CAREER
    Apply for roles through RoleCast, nail callback interviews, survive chemistry reads, and get cast. Master 6 film genres to unlock bigger budgets and bigger paychecks.

    ★ FILM FRANCHISES
    Hit movies spawn sequel offers with escalating pay. Rival NPCs run their own franchises — beat them to the #1 opening weekend or watch them steal your spotlight.

    ★ MANAGE YOUR EMPIRE
    • StarBank — savings with weekly interest, investment portfolio with dividends, loans, full transaction history
    • Forbes list — rank against every other celebrity; power couples merge into joint net worth
    • Awards season — campaign for Oscars, Globes and more across a full year
    • Endorsements — recurring sponsor deals that pay out every week

    ★ RIVALS, SCANDALS & TABLOID DRAMA
    • Rival of the Year — NPC careers rise and fall; head-to-head scoring on every role and award
    • Scandal arcs — 3-week crisis cycles with 6 PR response options, paparazzi ambushes, and a PR Manager who flags mistakes
    • SpotlightX — social feed with posts, DMs, communities, and beef threads

    ★ LOVE, MARRIAGE & MESSY BREAKUPS
    Build real relationships — crush, dating, engaged, married. Every love interest has a unique archetype. Breakups end amicably or explode into scandal. Power couples merge into a single tabloid name.

    ★ MANAGERS & AGENTS WITH OPINIONS
    Your team has a strategy — fame, prestige, paycheck, indie, or legacy. Take roles that don't align and approval drops. Veto twice and your manager walks. Real contracts with exclusivity and early-exit penalties.

    ★ STARCHAIN (IN-GAME CRYPTO)
    A fully simulated crypto market with coins, NFTs, and gems. Follow whale signals, subscribe to Copy-Trade Pro, stake, borrow against your bag, pay quarterly taxes. No real money, no real blockchain.

    ★ BUILT FOR YOUR PHONE
    Full iOS-style phone UI inside the game: live status bar, notification badges, energy-bound battery, glassy dock. Every system lives in a dedicated app.

    ★ CREATOR MODE (OPTIONAL PAID UNLOCK)
    One-time $9.99 in-app purchase. Unlocks a god-mode panel with 80+ actions: adjust cash, fame, and stats, skip weeks, force scandals, trigger storylines. For players who want a sandbox after beating the career loop.

    ★ 100% OFFLINE & PRIVATE
    • No internet required after install
    • No ads, ever
    • No accounts, no login
    • No analytics, no tracking
    • All saves stay on your device

    One-time optional purchase. No subscriptions. No energy meters. No pay-to-win. Just a deep, quiet, single-player life sim you can play on a plane.
