    Official description

    Two combat styles to seamlessly switch between in freeflowing combat.
    A next-level stylish action RPG.

    ★ Open Beta Test ★
    Join up as a Guardian and be one of the first to experience ARES: THE IRON VANGUARD.

    Your feedback will help us shape the game for launch.

    ▣ Game Introduction ▣

    ■ Suit-Switching: Change the Flow of Battle ■
    Switch between two suits in real time. Combine weapons and skills to create your own combat style.
    Single-handedly turn the tide of war, and dominate hordes of enemies in style.

    ■ Wield Multiple Classes in Combat ■
    The Engineer: fight with heavy gauntlets and deploy drone assaults.
    The Warlock: unleash AoE magic powered by cosmic energy.
    The Warlord: brave the front lines with a massive rocket-hammer and shield.
    The Hunter: cut through the enemy with swift dual blades.
    Multi-class: Mix and match different suits for endless possibilities.

    ■ Unmatched Firepower & Mobility ■
    Glide over the open battlefield on a hover board.
    Call down and pilot your Mech: the Goliath.
    Reclaim the airspace with the Valkyrie!

    ■ Multiplayer Co-op ■
    Experience the thrill of teamwork-driven combat through raids, strike missions, and more.
    Fight end-game bosses with friends.

    ■ Stylish Action On the Go■
    An action RPG where timing and skill decide everything!
    Experience console-quality gameplay that combines parries, perfect-dodges, and combos.

    ■ You’re the Solar System’s Last Hope ■
    A grand journey unfolds across vast battlefields.
    Become a Guardian fighting against the threat of Nemesis and launch into the center of a new war to protect the solar system.
