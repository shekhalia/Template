    Official description

    Welcome to Clash of Realms, a unique blend of tactical semi-auto-battler strategy, roguelike deckbuilding, and kingdom management. Step into a world where endless scaling meets infinite strategy, and where your creativity is your greatest weapon.
    Draw your cards, place them strategically on the grid, and watch your kingdom come alive before the auto-battle begins. But the fight doesn't end there: stay active and turn the tides of war with game-changing spells and abilities!
    🏰 BUILD YOUR GRID KINGDOM Your grid is your fortress. Place your cards wisely on the beautiful 2D isometric battlefield. Draft and place a variety of cards including frontline troops, defensive towers, scale buildings, and powerful buffs. Positioning, team composition, and unit synergies are the keys to victory.
    ⚔️ CHOOSE YOUR REALM Command unique civilizations, each with entirely different visual and mechanical experiences. Mix and match abilities to create unexpected and unstoppable synergies:
    🧛 Vampires: Dominate through lifesteal and dark magic. Use the Blood Fountain to permanently grow the health of your units or unleash Vampires to feast on the fallen.
    💥 Cannoneers: Overwhelm the enemy with pure firepower. Deploy Fedai assassins to pierce through the frontline while your Musketeers pick off the weakest targets.
    ⚒️ Dwarves: Master the economy and gold-scaling strategies. Generate massive wealth with Banks and use the Hand of Midas to turn the tide of battle into a golden opportunity.
    💀 Undead: Overrun the battlefield with an endless swarm. Use the Undead Citadel to spawn zombies periodically and raise fallen allies from the Graveyard to fight again as part of your immortal army.
    ⚡ SEMI-AUTO-BATTLER: CAST POWERFUL SPELLS Unlike traditional auto-battlers, you are never just a spectator. Actively influence the clash using real-time spells. Cast a well-timed Divine Shield to protect your carry unit, trigger Berserk to shred the enemy boss, or summon emergency reinforcements. Your timing decides victory or defeat.
    🔥 ENDLESS SCALING & PERKS Experimentation is key. Clash of Realms is designed to let you break the game with your own imagination. Discover general and race-specific Perks to modify your run. The core loop is dangerously addictive: Draw, Place, Scale, Survive!
    💎 FAIR PROGRESSION Gems and gold earned from levels allow players to upgrade their civilization's cards. Earn gold & gems during your runs to strengthen your forces. Progression is fair and accessible. Spending accelerates progress but does not gate it, ensuring nothing blocks core content.
