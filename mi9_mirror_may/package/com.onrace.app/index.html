    Official description

    OnRace is a GPS lap timing and performance tracking app built for motorsport enthusiasts.

    Designed for riders and drivers, OnRace lets you record, analyze, and share your racing sessions across multiple disciplines:
    • Motorcycle track days
    • Motocross
    • Karting
    • Car track sessions

    ⏱️ ACCURATE LAP TIMING
    Record your sessions using GPS and automatically detect laps on supported circuits.
    Analyze lap times, best laps, total time, distance, and key performance metrics.

    📍 GPS TRACK & SESSION DATA
    Visualize your GPS trace on the track and review your session data after each ride or drive.
    Understand where you gain or lose time and improve session after session.

    📊 PERFORMANCE ANALYSIS
    Compare laps, review statistics, and track your progress over time.
    Designed for training, testing, and competitive practice.

    🤝 SHARE YOUR SESSIONS
    Add photos to your activities and share your sessions with friends.
    Follow other riders and drivers and explore their performances.

    🏁 MADE FOR MOTORSPORT
    OnRace is built specifically for motorsport, with a focus on simplicity, speed, and real-world track use.
    Start tracking for free and upgrade when you want deeper insights.

    OnRace helps you ride faster, drive smarter, and get better every session.
