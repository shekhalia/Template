    Official description

    StudentAI is a curriculum-aligned AI tutor and study companion designed for what students are taught in class and tested on in exams. While many AI tools give generic answers, StudentAI is built to help you learn the way your school, college, or exam board expects, so explanations and study guides feel relevant to what you are working on right now.

    From SATs and ACTs to AP, IB, GCSE, A-Levels, university coursework, and more, StudentAI lets you tailor answers to your syllabus, exam board, and grade level. Snap a photo, type a question, or speak it out loud, and get clear, step-by-step explanations you can actually learn from.

    WHY STUDENTS CHOOSE STUDENTAI

    - CURRICULUM-ALIGNED ANSWERS: Pick your country, school level, exam board, or course and get explanations tailored to your syllabus.

    - AI HOMEWORK HELPER: Get step-by-step solutions for math, science, history, English, coding, business, and more, presented in formats commonly used in class.

    - ESSAY WRITER and EDITOR: Brainstorm ideas, generate outlines, draft essays, paraphrase, summarize, and check grammar with rubrics in mind.

    - MATH SOLVER: Algebra, calculus, statistics, geometry, and physics solved with clear methods aligned to common textbook approaches.

    - STUDY GUIDES and NOTES: Upload lecture notes, PDFs, or textbook chapters and instantly get summaries, flashcards, mind maps, and study guides aligned to your course outcomes.

    - EXAM and QUIZ PREP: Generate unlimited practice questions designed to cover topics commonly seen in SAT, ACT, AP, IB, GCSE, A-Level, MCAT, LSAT, GRE, and more.

    - AI TUTOR CHAT: Ask follow-up questions, get unlimited explanations, and learn at your own pace with a tutor that understands your curriculum context.

    - LANGUAGE LEARNING: Translate, learn vocabulary, practice grammar, and improve writing in 50+ languages, with content mapped to common school language curricula.

    - CITATIONS and RESEARCH: Find sources, generate citations in APA, MLA, Chicago, and Harvard formats, and write research papers with confidence.

    BUILT FOR EVERY STUDENT AND EVERY CURRICULUM

    - High school students preparing for SATs, ACTs, AP exams, GCSEs, A-Levels, IB, and finals
    - College and university students tackling assignments, essays, and degree-level coursework
    - Graduate students working on dissertations and prepping for MCAT, LSAT, GRE
    - Adult learners returning to study or upskilling for certifications
    - Self-learners exploring new subjects with structured, syllabus-style guidance

    WHY STUDENTS LIKE STUDENTAI

    * Curriculum-aware: tailored to your country, school, exam board, and grade
    * Designed to cover topics from common past papers, mark schemes, and standard textbooks
    * Step-by-step explanations, not just final answers, so you actually learn
    * Snap-to-solve photo input for handwriting, textbooks, and screenshots
    * Voice input for hands-free studying
    * Powered by modern AI models, regularly updated
    * Clean, distraction-free interface designed for focus
    * Private and secure: your work stays yours
    * Available anytime, anywhere: a study helper in your pocket

    SUBSCRIPTION AND PRICING

    StudentAI offers a free tier so you can try it risk-free. Upgrade to StudentAI Pro for unlimited curriculum-aligned questions, advanced AI models, faster responses, and premium study tools. Subscriptions auto-renew unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel anytime in your Apple ID settings.
