    Official description

    Blood Sugar Tracker & Logbook helps you track blood sugar, blood pressure, weight, and estimated A1C in one easy health logbook.

    Record daily readings, view charts and trends, set reminders, and export reports for your personal records or doctor visits. Whether you need a blood sugar tracker, diabetes logbook, BP tracker, or weight log, this app keeps your health data organized and easy to review.

    Track your key health metrics

    • Blood Sugar / Glucose
    • Blood Pressure (BP)
    • Pulse
    • Weight
    • Estimated A1C

    Key Features

    Blood Sugar Logging
    Log blood glucose readings with helpful timing tags such as fasting, before meals, and after meals. Add notes to keep more detailed records.

    Blood Pressure & Pulse Logs
    Track systolic and diastolic blood pressure along with pulse readings in one place.

    Weight & BMI Tracking
    Record weight entries and monitor BMI using your saved profile details.

    Estimated A1C Overview
    See an estimated A1C based on your logged glucose data for general awareness and progress tracking.

    Charts & Trends
    View easy-to-read charts for blood sugar, blood pressure, and weight to understand changes over time.

    Reminders
    Set optional reminders to help maintain a regular logging routine.

    Export Reports
    Export your data in PDF or CSV format for personal records or to share during medical consultations.

    Flexible Units
    Supports blood glucose units in mg/dL and mmol/L, and weight units in kg and lbs.

    Quick & Easy Logging
    Add readings quickly so daily tracking becomes simple and consistent.

    Optional Sign-In & Sync
    Sign in to sync your data across devices while keeping your information private and under your control.

    Important Disclaimer

    This app is intended for personal tracking and informational purposes only. It does not measure blood sugar, blood pressure, or weight, and it does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always seek guidance from a qualified healthcare professional regarding any medical condition or health concern.
