    Official description

    Square Cloud is a cloud hosting platform designed to simplify the management of applications, bots, APIs, and websites in a fast, secure, and stable way. With the official app, developers can monitor and control their entire infrastructure directly from their phone, without depending on constant access to a computer or terminal commands.

    The app offers complete control over your hosting, with access to application status, real-time execution logs, CPU and memory usage graphs, and all the essential actions needed to keep your services online. You can start, restart, stop, or update projects with a single tap, adjust environment variables and domains remotely, and create snapshots or restore databases whenever needed. You can also keep track of your usage, limits, and billing history, keeping everything under control.

    Square Cloud takes care of the cloud infrastructure, ensuring performance, stability, and high availability for your projects, while you focus on what really matters: developing and evolving your applications. Designed to support multiple languages and different types of projects, the platform serves both those deploying their first bot and experienced developers managing more complex architectures in production.

    Whether for automation bots, websites, APIs, or projects in development, the app delivers a modern cloud hosting experience, letting you monitor and manage everything in real time, from anywhere.
