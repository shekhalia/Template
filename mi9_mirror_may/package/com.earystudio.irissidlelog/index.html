    Official description

    Iris's Idle Log is an idle RPG about growth, crafting, exploration, and auto-battle progression.

    Follow Iris as her small adventure log expands into a journey across new regions, resources, skills, and discoveries. Instead of demanding constant control, the game focuses on meaningful preparation: choose what to craft, what to train, what to collect, and how far to push your next expedition.

    Battles play out automatically, but your choices still matter. Prepare equipment, food, and skills before heading out, then decide whether to keep advancing or retreat safely with your loot. A successful return secures your rewards, while pushing too far can turn risk into loss. Each short session gives you a clear decision, and every upgrade helps you reach farther next time.

    Progress is not limited to combat. Cooking, farming, crafting, smithing, training, meditation, resting, and other non-combat skills all support your long-term growth. Gather materials, make useful items, improve your stats, and build your own progression route step by step.

    Offline rewards and repeatable idle systems let your character keep growing even when you are away. Check in, collect progress, plan your next actions, and continue your adventure at your own pace.

    Write a quiet but steady adventure log with Iris and her mysterious companion, Bibi.
    What will you gather today? What will you craft next? How far can your next expedition go?

    Start your own idle adventure in Iris's Idle Log.
