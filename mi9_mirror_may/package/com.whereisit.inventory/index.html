    Official description

    Stop asking "Where is it?" and start finding it.

    WhereIsIt is the ultimate home inventory and organization tracker designed to help you catalogue your belongings, track valuables for insurance, and collaborate with your family. Whether you are moving, organizing a storage unit, or just want peace of mind, WhereIsIt makes decluttering effortless.

    ✨ Key Features

    📸 AI-Powered Shelf Analysis
    Don't type everything manually! Simply snap a photo of a shelf or open box, and our advanced AI (powered by Google Gemini) automatically identifies and lists items for you. Perfect for cataloging pantry items, books, and storage bins in seconds.

    🏠 Household Sync & Collaboration
    Manage your home together.

    Create a Household: Set up a shared space for your family.
    Invite Members: Share a simple 6-character code to let family members join.
    Real-Time Sync: Changes made on one device instantly update for everyone.
    📄 Insurance-Ready Reports
    Protect your net worth. With a single tap, generate a professional PDF Insurance Report complete with item photos, descriptions, and estimated values. Export directly to your phone to share with insurance agents.

    🔍 Powerful Search & Organization

    Organize by Room, Container, or Category.
    Global search lets you find that specific cable or holiday decoration instantly.
    View Analytics to track total item counts and inventory value.
    🛡️ Secure & Offline-First
    Your data belongs to you. WhereIsIt functions completely offline, storing data locally on your device for maximum speed. When you're back online, we securely sync your changes to the cloud so you never lose your data.

    🚀 Premium Features

    Unlimited Items & Photos
    Advanced AI Scanning Modes
    Priority Cloud Sync
    Extended Export Options (CSV & PDF)
    Who is this for?

    Homeowners: Track warranty dates and expensive appliances.
    Movers: Know exactly which box contains your kitchenware.
    Collectors: Catalog collections with photos and details.
    Small Businesses: Track lightweight inventory and supplies.
    Download WhereIsIt today and turn chaos into order!
