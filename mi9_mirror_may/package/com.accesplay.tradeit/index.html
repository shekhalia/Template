    Official description

    Tradeit is the all-in-one app for in-person trading card communities. It helps players, collectors, and local game stores organize fair, transparent trades and connect through nearby events.

    Built for face-to-face trading, Tradeit makes each exchange easier to manage from start to finish: from adding cards and comparing offers to confirming final agreements. Instead of juggling notes, screenshots, and messages, you get a shared structure that keeps every trade clear for both sides.

    For players and collectors, Tradeit lets you:

    Run live in-person trade sessions with a clean, guided flow.
    Add and review cards on each side of a trade in real time.
    Use estimated card values to make more informed decisions.
    Confirm deals with both parties so there is no confusion.
    Keep your trading activity organized for future reference.
    Discover nearby trading events and join local communities.
    For stores and organizers, Tradeit is also a growth and promotion tool:

    Register your store and create a public presence for local traders.
    Create in-store or community trade events in minutes.
    Promote upcoming events to nearby players and collectors.
    Share event details like date, time, location, and format.
    Build recurring trade nights and increase repeat foot traffic.
    Reach people actively looking for card trading opportunities.
    Whether you host a weekly trade night, run a multi-game TCG community, or just want better one-on-one trades, Tradeit is designed to support real-world trading culture.

    Why Tradeit:

    In-person first: designed around live, face-to-face trading.
    Clear and fair: both sides can review and confirm before closing a deal.
    Community driven: discover events and connect with nearby traders.
    Store friendly: create, promote, and grow local trading events.
    Tradeit supports communities across collectible card games where people meet, trade, and grow together.

    Download Tradeit and make every in-person trade smoother, safer, and more rewarding.
