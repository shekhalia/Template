    Official description

    Image Compressor & Resizer is a fast and simple tool that helps you reduce image size and resize photos without sacrificing quality.

    Whether you need to save storage, share photos faster, or meet upload limits for websites and forms, this app lets you compress and resize images in seconds.

    Designed for speed and simplicity, the app works completely offline and processes images directly on your device.

    Key Features

    High-Quality Image Compression
    Reduce image file size significantly while maintaining visual quality.

    Resize Images Instantly
    Change image dimensions to any size you need for social media, websites, or documents.

    Batch Processing
    Compress or resize multiple images at once to save time.

    Custom Compression Settings
    Choose the exact compression level and output resolution.

    Preview Before Saving
    See the estimated file size before exporting the image.

    Offline Processing
    All compression happens locally on your device. No internet required and your images stay private.

    Fast & Lightweight
    Optimized for performance so images process quickly even on large files.
