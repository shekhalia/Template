    Official description

    Cow Way: A Delicious Hay Party with Cute Cows!

    Welcome to the peaceful farm, where hungry cows are waiting for a treat! Cow Way is a unique stack & queue management puzzle game. Strategically place colorful hay bales to feed these adorable cows and keep them happy!

    It's not just about placing hay. You must manage the stack of waiting cows by checking their color order and map out the perfect 'feeding path' on the limited board space. Experience the satisfying thrill of watching cows rush out to eat their matching hay, clearing the busy farm one bale at a time!

    Key Features
    New Puzzle Mechanic: A fresh blend of line-drawing hay placement and intelligent stack management based on cow color order.

    Cuteness Overload: Enjoy a peaceful farm setting with round, cheerful cows that are just too cute to resist.

    Brain-Teasing Strategy: Plan ahead to ensure your path isn't blocked and that each cow can reach its matching hay bale.

    Stress-Free Healing: No time limits! Relax, think, and solve puzzles at your own pace for a truly calming experience.

    Diverse Levels: Confront increasingly interesting board layouts and color combinations that challenge your wits.

    How to Play
    Check the Queue: Look at the bottom of the screen to see the color order of the waiting cows.

    Place Hay Bales: Plan a path and place the provided hay bales on the board in a line-drawing style. Remember to keep a clear way for the cows!

    Delicious Snack Time: When a waiting cow sees a matching hay bale at the front, it dashes out to eat it up.

    Clear the Farm: Successfully manage the flow so all hay bales get eaten and the farm is clear to win!

    Forget the hustle and bustle of daily life for a while. Spend some peaceful puzzle time on the Cow Way farm. Your smart strategy will bring joy to these hungry cows and leave you with a perfectly tidy pasture!

    Download now and start your delicious hay-sorting journey with the cutest cows!
