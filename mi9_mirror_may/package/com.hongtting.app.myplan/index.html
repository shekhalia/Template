    Official description

    1. Development Philosophy: "Making Complex Life Simple"
    Modern people are very busy with many tasks and projects. To organize this mess, My Plan was made with two main ideas: being easy to use and growing with you. The developer made this tool for their own family and life. That is why it has great details that solve the real problems we face every day.

    2. Main Features: Data and Great Experience
    Safe Data Storage: All data is saved directly in your web browser. You can write your plans in a safe place without worrying about losing your private information.

    Scroll Calendar: We don't use old, boring box-style calendars. Instead, we show a scrolling layout so you can see time flow smoothly. This helps you understand how the past and future are connected.

    Project Groups: This is not just a simple "To-Do" list. You can group your tasks into projects. This helps you set clear priorities for work, self-study, and family events.

    Easy Sharing with JSON: You can manage your data easily using JSON files. You can back up your data, and you can also bring in projects shared by others. This makes working together and sharing very easy.
