    Official description

    Master Biology with BioQuiz — the #1 offline biology quiz app for students, teachers, and science enthusiasts! Whether you're preparing for your biology exam, reviewing high school or college biology topics, or just love life science, BioQuiz makes studying engaging, effective, and completely free of internet dependency.

    ### Why BioQuiz?

    BioQuiz combines interactive multiple-choice questions with a gamified learning system, covering all major biology topics: Cell Biology, Genetics, DNA & RNA, Evolution, Ecology, Human Anatomy, Microbiology, Plant Biology, and more. Each question comes with detailed scientific explanations, so you don't just get the right answer — you understand the biology behind it.

    ### Key Features

    📶 **100% Offline Biology Study**
    - Full functionality without Wi-Fi or mobile data
    - All biology content pre-loaded — no extra downloads ever
    - Study biology on the subway, on trips, in rural areas, or anywhere with no signal

    📚 **Comprehensive Biology Content**
    - Hundreds of biology quiz questions across multiple thematic modules
    - Topics: Cell Biology, Genetics & Heredity, DNA, Evolution, Ecology & Environment, Microbiology, Human Body Systems, Plant Biology, and more
    - Multiple difficulty levels: beginner, intermediate, and advanced
    - Detailed explanations for every answer — right or wrong — to reinforce learning

    🎮 **Gamified Biology Learning**
    - Earn XP points and unlock biology achievement badges
    - Track your progress with detailed performance statistics
    - Review your answer history to identify weak spots and improve
    - Progressive challenges that adapt to your biology knowledge level

    ### Perfect For

    - High school and college biology students studying for exams and vestibular
    - Biology teachers looking for a quiz tool for classroom engagement
    - Science enthusiasts wanting to test their life science knowledge
    - Anyone preparing for biology-focused entrance exams, ENEM, SAT, or competitive tests

    ### Biology Topics Covered

    Cell structure and function • Genetics and Mendelian inheritance • DNA replication and protein synthesis • Natural selection and evolutionary biology • Ecosystem dynamics and ecology • Microbiology and bacteria • Human anatomy and physiology • Plant biology and photosynthesis • Biodiversity and taxonomy • Biochemistry fundamentals
