    Official description

    Welcome to Neon Dodge, the ultimate arcade challenge that will test your reflexes! Immerse yourself in a retro-futuristic 3D grid, dodge obstacles at breakneck speeds, and prove you have what it takes to survive.

    🚀 KEY FEATURES 🚀

    ⚡ TWO GAME MODES:
    • Classic Mode: Warm up your engines, collect coins, and get ready for a progressive speed increase.

    • Extreme Mode: Think you're an expert? Jump straight into top speed. Only for true ninjas!

    🛸 ASSEMBLE YOUR TEAM (SUPPORT DRONES): Don't fly alone. Buy and equip Drones in the shop to help you on your mission:
    • Collector Drone: Will grab distant coins for you.

    • Sentry Drone: Will fire its laser to destroy the red blocks that threaten your life.

    🌈 BONUS DIMENSION (FEVER MODE):
    Collect the hidden letters N-E-O-N during gameplay to activate Fever Mode. The grid will transform into a rainbow tunnel, the music will intensify, and hundreds of coins will rain down with no enemies in sight. Pure euphoria!

    🎁 DAILY REWARDS AND MISSIONS:
    Play every day to maintain your streak. Complete all 7 days to unlock the exclusive VIP Gold Ship with a built-in shield! Plus, complete rotating missions to earn extra coins.

    🎧 AUDIOVISUAL SYNESTHESIA:
    Enjoy an electronic (Synthwave) soundtrack that reacts to your gameplay. The music speeds up with the difficulty, and the graphics pulse to the bassline.
