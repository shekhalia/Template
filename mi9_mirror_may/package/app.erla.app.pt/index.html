    Official description

    You're not bad at languages. Your method was.

    If you've been studying Portuguese but still can't understand native speakers — the problem isn't you. Erla is a Portuguese learning app built on a simple truth: real comprehension comes from listening, not flashcards.

    🎧 LEARN PORTUGUESE BY LISTENING
    Hear real Portuguese sentences, then reveal the text to check your understanding. Adjust speed, read grammar explanations, and rate your confidence — all in under a minute. This is how you train your ear to understand Portuguese.

    📖 READ SHORT STORIES IN PORTUGUESE
    Tap any sentence to see its translation and grammar breakdown. Build vocabulary through context — no word lists, no drills. Just real Portuguese reading practice that sticks.

    🎬 WATCH AND UNDERSTAND PORTUGUESE
    Short videos play without subtitles first — testing your real Portuguese listening skills. Subtitles appear on the second play. It's Portuguese listening practice meets short-form video.

    💬 SPEAK PORTUGUESE WITH AN AI TEACHER (PRO)
    Practice speaking Portuguese in a safe, judgment-free space. Choose from 12 real-life topics and get a detailed report after each session: score, grammar corrections, and vocabulary in context.

    ✅ WHY ERLA IS DIFFERENT
    Erla is built on natural language acquisition — the way you learned your first language. You listen, absorb, and understand. Every session takes just 5-10 minutes. Your daily goal, streak calendar, and understanding score show exactly how far you've come.

    📊 FEATURES
    - Grammar explanations for every sentence
    - Translations in your native language
    - Portuguese listening practice with adjustable speed
    - Spaced repetition based on performance
    - Understanding score from your last 100 lessons
    - Daily goals from 20 to 300 points
    - Portuguese for beginners, intermediate, and advanced learners
    - Offline support
