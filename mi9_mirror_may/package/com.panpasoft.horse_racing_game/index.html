    • Champions Cup invitational — the ultimate showdown
    • Prize pools reaching $1,000,000+

    🧬 BREEDING & GENETICS
    ═══════════════════════════════════

    The future of your stable is in the bloodlines.

    • Pair stallions with mares to create the next generation
    • Inherit Speed, Stamina, Acceleration potential
    • Random variations: legend foals can outperform both parents
    • Racing style inheritance across 4 strategies:
    Front Runner • Stalker • Mid-Pack • Closer
    • Yearling Sale Catalog for premium offspring

    💰 MARKET ECONOMY

    • Make custom offers with cooldown timers
    • Build wealth through prize money, sponsors, and smart trading

    • Sponsor activity bonuses by race tier

    Your champions live forever.

    • Retired horses become breeding studs
    • Family trees track generations
    • Legacy management at age 8+
    • Memorial wall for deceased champions

    ═══════════════════════════════════

    • Optional rewarded ads for daily bonus cash
