    Patna Metro Navigator – Offline Metro Map & Route Planner

    Plan your metro journey in Patna with ease – no internet required!
    Patna Metro Navigator is your all-in-one offline metro guide, helping you find the best routes, explore station details, and locate nearby stations. Built with a clean design and bilingual support, it ensures a smooth travel experience for locals and visitors alike.

    ✨ Key Features

    🚇 Smart Route Planner
    • Find the fastest route between any two stations
    • Handles both direct and interchange routes
    • Get travel time, number of stops, and estimated fare
    • Color-coded lines for easy identification

    🏙️ Station Information
    • First/last train timings, facilities, and parking info
    • Station names in English & Hindi
    • Icons for escalators, lifts, ATMs, restrooms, and parking
    • Interchange stations clearly marked

    🗺️ Interactive Metro Map
    • Google Maps integration with metro overlay
    • Custom markers for stations & interchanges
    • Official metro line colors for easy navigation
    • Tap a station to view quick info

    📍 Nearby Stations
    • Find stations within a 2km radius using GPS
    • Sorted by distance for convenience
    • Get directions via Google Maps
    • Real-time location updates

    🌗 Theme Support
    • Light & dark modes with auto detection
    • Option to switch manually
    • Consistent, modern design

    🈳 Multilingual Support
    • Available in English & Hindi
    • Bilingual station details
    • Switch language anytime from settings

    Whether you’re a daily commuter or a first-time visitor, Patna Metro Navigator makes metro travel simple, fast, and reliable – all without using mobile data.
