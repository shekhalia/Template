    Official description

    TEDI by Ensemble is the communication app from Ensemble Health Partners, a leading healthcare revenue cycle management company serving hospitals, health systems and physician practices nationwide.

    Designed for associates, TEDI by Ensemble makes it easy to stay connected to what's happening across our organization-anytime, anywhere.
    Through the public areas of the app, users can explore company updates, upcoming events, and career opportunities, all in one convenient place.

    With TEDI by Ensemble, you can:
    Stay informed with the latest company news and updates Discover events through an easy-to-browse event calendar
    Get to know Ensemble through highlights and stories that bring our culture to life
    TEDI by Ensemble keeps you connected to the moments, milestones and opportunities that matter most-mobile, simple and always up to date.
