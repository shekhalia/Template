    Official description

    Chat in any language with AI auto-translation!

    OctoChat is a secure messaging app that puts your privacy first. Every message, photo, video, and file is encrypted end-to-end using
    XChaCha20-Poly1305 encryption before it ever leaves your device.

    KEY FEATURES

    Encrypted Messaging
    Send text messages, voice notes, photos, videos, and documents — all fully encrypted. Only you and your recipient can read them.

    AI Writing Assistant
    Write better with AI-powered tools. Proofread your messages for spelling and grammar, or enhance them in three styles: Professional, Casual,
    or Friendly. Every message you send sounds exactly the way you want it.

    Real-Time Translation
    Break every language barrier. OctoChat automatically detects who speaks which language and translates messages in real time — both voice and
    text — into any language. Every participant sees the conversation in their own language. No manual setup needed.

    Concierge Service for Businesses
    Perfect for hotels, restaurants, and service providers. Share your business QR code with customers — they download OctoChat for free (your
    business covers the cost). Communicate with guests in any language with professional, perfectly translated messages. Turn language barriers
    into seamless service experiences.

    Invite Friends Easily
    Share a QR code or invite link to connect with friends instantly. Getting started is fast and simple.

    Multi-Device Sync
    Seamlessly switch between your phone, tablet, and desktop. Your conversations stay in sync across all your devices.

    Offline Support
    Keep chatting even without an internet connection. Messages are stored locally and synced automatically when you're back online.

    Document Sharing
    Share files and documents securely with encrypted previews. Your shared files are protected with the same strong encryption as your messages.

    PRIVACY BY DESIGN

    OctoChat is built from the ground up with security in mind. We use XChaCha20-Poly1305 encryption for all data. Your messages are yours — we
    can't read them, and neither can anyone else.

    AI translation supports any language. The app interface is available in English, German, French, and Spanish.
