    Official description

    Dive into the fascinating world of the human body with bodyxq.

    See how organs work, how diseases develop, and how micronutrients influence your health. With vivid 3D visuals and clear explanations, bodyxq helps you understand what is happening inside your body and why it matters.

    Explore the heart and the circulatory system in impressive detail. Zoom from whole organs down to the level of billions of cells and follow the processes that keep you alive. Learn how conditions such as atherosclerosis, high blood pressure, and heart failure can develop, and understand the role that vitamins, minerals, amino acids, and trace elements play in keeping cells healthy and functional.

    Bodyxq is fully interactive. Open a beating heart right on your smartphone or tablet, travel through the coronary arteries, and see where risks can arise. Watch how nutrient deficiencies may lead to disease and discover what supports long term health. You will also experience why a heart attack can happen, while a nose attack cannot.

    Bodyxq is for everyone who wants to understand health better. It is a powerful learning tool for students and teachers, and a practical resource for doctors, therapists, and coaches who want to explain complex topics in a simple and engaging way.

    Turn knowledge into better health for yourself and the people you care about. Install bodyxq now and gain a completely new perspective on the human body.
