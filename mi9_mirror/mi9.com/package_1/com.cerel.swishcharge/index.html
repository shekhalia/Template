    Official description

    Swish is a mobile electric vehicle charging service. Recharge faster, easier and smarter.
    Swish's EV mobile charging service lets you view and manage charging remotely. With the same account, charge your vehicle at all Swish or public charging points - whether you are at home, at work or on the go.
    Just plug in your car, we'll take care of the rest. From the app, you can:
    - Consult the real-time map of the status of the charging points (Available - Charging in progress - Out of service).
    - Reserve a charging point in advance.
    - Start and stop charging.
    - Monitor charging remotely.
    In addition to our charging network, users can charge their vehicle across Europe with their Swish account, thanks to our roaming partners!
    To charge your vehicle with the Swish app, you must be a registered user. Register at: https://swishforgood.register.virtaglobal.com/register
    The service is prepaid, the amount of each recharge will be automatically billed to you based on the prices of each charging station. When checking in, you will have to pay an amount of 30 euros, which will be credited to your account.
