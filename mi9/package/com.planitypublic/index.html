    Planity offers a practical way to book beauty and barber appointments across a large network of salons. The core function is straightforward, letting users browse services, compare prices, and secure a slot directly from their phone. This can simplify the process of fitting a haircut or manicure into a busy schedule.

    Feedback suggests the booking system itself is generally effective when it works. Users appreciate the ability to manage or reschedule appointments from their account and the reminders sent before a booking. However, some encounter technical friction, such as screens not loading properly or registration delays with SMS codes. The experience may depend on your device and location.

    For someone regularly booking beauty services in its supported regions, Planity provides a centralized catalog. It is most suitable for those willing to navigate potential interface quirks for the convenience of online booking.
