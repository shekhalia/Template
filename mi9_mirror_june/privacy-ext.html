Privacy Policy

Android Apps Old Versions is a Chrome extension that helps users view and access old versions of Android apps directly from their Google Play details page.

Information We Collect

This extension does not collect, store, or share any personal or sensitive user data.

How the Extension Works

  • The extension reads the URL of the active browser tab only to detect the app ID (?id=...) when you are on a Google Play details page.
  • It fetches publicly available app information (title, icon, description) from Google Play.
  • It retrieves version history data from versions.playstoreapi.com and provides links to download pages.

What We Do Not Collect

This extension does not access or collect:

  • Personally identifiable information (such as name, email, or address)
  • Health, financial, or authentication information
  • Personal communications
  • Precise location data
  • Browsing history or user activity outside of the extension’s single purpose

Data Sharing

No personal data is collected, stored, or shared. The extension does not sell or transfer any information to third parties.
